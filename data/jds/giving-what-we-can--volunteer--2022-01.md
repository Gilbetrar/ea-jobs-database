# Giving

### [Take a giving pledge](/pledge)

We invite you to join our community of 10,732  people who have taken a pledge to donate a portion of their income to highly effective causes.

### [Donate to effective charities](/best-charities-to-donate-to-2026)

If you're not quite ready to pledge, why not start by making one donation to an effective charity?

### [Create a fundraising page](/fundraisers)

Start fundraising for high-impact charities amongst your friends, family or colleagues. The GWWC team will help set up a custom page for you.

### [Make a bequest](/get-involved/bequests)

Donating money from your estate to effective charities could help you to leave behind an incredible legacy. Leaving a gift in your will to charity is typically called a bequest, but can also be known as planned giving.

# Community

### [Join our online community](/get-involved/connect-to-community)

We have a community slack workspace designed to help you connect with others interested in effective giving, so you can self-organise meetups, ask questions and much more!

### [Attend one of our events](/events)

We run regular events (mostly online) where you can meet people in the Giving What We Can community from around the world, hear what's going on, discuss effective giving, and more.

# Advocacy

### [Spread the word](/get-involved/share-our-ideas)

Help us spread the word about giving more and giving more effectively. Talking to friends or family about these topics may be one of the best ways to help; advocating for effective giving multiplies your impact!

[Share our ideas](/get-involved/share-our-ideas)

[Share your commitment with our advocacy assets](/get-involved/advocacy-assets)

### [Host a giving event](/events/guides)

[Giving games](/events/guides/giving-games), [everyday philanthropist events](/events/guides/everyday-philanthropist), and [pledge celebrations](/events/guides/pledge-celebration) are fantastic ways to get more involved -- and to get people talking and learning about effective giving in a fun, engaging, and interactive way.

# More ways to make a difference

While donating effectively is one of the most powerful ways to make a difference, it’s not the only one.

We encourage you to think broadly about how you can use your time, skills, and career to create a positive impact. Your career choices can be an incredible force for good — if you’d like to explore this further, we recommend checking out [80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/) and [Probably Good](https://probablygood.org/) for practical guidance on how to align your work with doing the most good.
