* These fellowships include:
* Visiting Fellowships: exploring ethics, policy, politics and quality of life within prevailing socio-technical systems.
* Doctoral Fellowship Program: engaging students in systematic inquiry into ethical and political implications of the digital age.
* Postdoc Fellowships: supporting research in ethics, politics, and quality of life in digital societies, on issues such as algorithmic decision systems, unfair discrimination, privacy, AI and ethics, and platform governance.
