* In this role, you'll lead a One for the World chapter at your MBA program to fight extreme poverty through effective giving.
* Build and lead a student volunteer team organizing events and outreach to promote The 1% Pledge.
* Dedicate 2-3 hours weekly to chapter activities, including regular calls with staff and semi-annual conferences.
* Educate peers about effective giving principles and GiveWell charities' impact.
* Raise funds for cost-effective charities by motivating fellow students to pledge 1% of their income.
