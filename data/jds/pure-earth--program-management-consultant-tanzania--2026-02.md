* In this role, you'll support the development of a lead poisoning prevention project in Tanzania through stakeholder engagement and operational planning.
* Identify and build relationships with key stakeholders across government, academia, and private sectors.
* Conduct a desk review analyzing Tanzania's "lead landscape" including health impacts and institutional capacity.
* Research operational requirements for Pure Earth in Tanzania and assist with initial staff recruitment.
* Develop a project work plan aligned with government priorities and Pure Earth's mission.
