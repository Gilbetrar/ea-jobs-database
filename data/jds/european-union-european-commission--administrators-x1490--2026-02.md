* This is an entry-level competition for a lifetime civil servant administrator role. You will be placed on a reserve list that institutions will draw from.
  In this role, you'll work as an administrator in EU institutions, drafting policies and implementing EU legislation.
* Analyze complex issues and provide policy advice to support decision-making processes.
* Draft reports, briefings, and correspondence in your designated policy area.
* Coordinate with stakeholders and represent your institution in meetings and working groups.
