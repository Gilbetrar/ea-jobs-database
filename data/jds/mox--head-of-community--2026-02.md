# **The Role in Context**
 

Mox is a community hub and coworking space in San Francisco's Mission District, founded in February 2025 to advance the cause of AI safety. Mox has 150+ active members, and has built a unique environment where researchers, advocates, builders, and innovators working on high-impact projects converge. Mox also hosts a thriving community center which caters primarily to niches in the SF tech scene such as the rationalist and effective altruist communities.
 

We are seeking a fulltime community manager to drive member growth and deepen Mox's connections across the SF tech ecosystem.
 

**This is a front-of-house role with a lot of surface area, with a reasonable workload and substantial support.** You’ll be doing outreach for private offices, managing connections with fellowship programs, facilitating volunteers and community event hosts, and publicizing Mox’s all while staying attentive to coworking member needs. You’ll be drawing on the strengths of everyone else on the team to make the Mox community shine.
 

In addition to the core responsibilities, the ideal candidate has a familiarity with SF tech culture, feels a sense of belonging in the Mox community, and the necessary context to grow into shaping Mox's overall strategy over time.
 

In this role more than any other on the team, you’ll have the opportunity to build lots of lasting relationships with our community members. The position holds key responsibilities in Mox’s overall strategy, and thus this position has the potential to grow into a leadership role at Mox.
 
# **Core Responsibilities**
 
## **Member Experience (40%)**
 
 
- Be a consistent presence for member questions and needs 
- Conduct new member onboarding and tours 
- Coordinate member-facing events that build connections and retain members such as weekly dinners, coffee chats, and community gatherings 
- Handle day-to-day member concerns (escalating to Rachel when needed) 
- Ensure members feel connected to the community and see value in membership 
 
## **Community Partnerships & Outreach (35%)**
 
 
- Recruit and coordinate volunteers and community event hosts 
- Outreach to potential private office tenants (companies, research groups, small orgs) 
- Manage relationships with fellowship programs (MATS, FLI, Apart, etc.) considering Mox for housing their cohorts
