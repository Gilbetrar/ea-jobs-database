* The Policy Fellowship is a two-year programme.
* Fellows spend up to five days at the University of Cambridge in the first year.
* The programme includes tailored one-to-one meetings with academic experts.
* Fellows build a personal network of over 1800 researchers and experts.
* Open to senior policy professionals from various sectors.
