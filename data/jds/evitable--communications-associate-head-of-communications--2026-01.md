# Communications Associate

# or Head of Communications
       
 
   
 
 

 

Title: Communications Associate or Head of Communications, depending on experience and qualifications

Location: San Francisco Bay Area, California (also considering remote Contractors)

Employment type: Full-time or Contract

Compensation: $80,000 - $110,000 (Communications Associate); $110,000 - $160,000 (Head of Communications) per annum, depending on experience and qualifications, with competitive benefits package

Reporting structure: Reports to CEO

Contact: [careers@evitable.com](mailto:careers@evitable.com?subject=Communications%20Associate%20or%20Head%20of%20Communications%20Inquiry)

Application form: [link](https://form.jotform.com/252827244190053)

Start date: Not fixed; hiring opportunistically

**About Evitable**

Evitable is an organization with a mission to make people aware of the dangers of AI, and aware that we can and should prevent further progress towards Superintelligence. 

**About Contract Roles**

We plan to work with contractors on a short-term (and potentially ongoing) basis to meet our immediate communications needs, which may include strategy, branding and narrative development, earned media and media training, media landscape mapping and tracking, content creation (written, web, video), and social media, grassroots, and/or advertising campaigns. Contractors may be remote, but we place a premium on working with potential long-term hires.

**About the Role**

For the full-time role, you will craft Evitable’s communications from the ground up. Drive awareness of AI's dangers and awareness that we can and should push back on A(S)I inevitability. Work directly with the CEO to design communication strategies and tactics, from branding to talking points, and proactively drive execution to deliver effectively on the Evitable mission. You will be required to work closely with the CEO, and be able to make quick decisions that represent their strategy and vision.

Coming in as one of the first hires means that you will be closely connected to our CEO and all aspects of our work--getting significant insight into our overall organizational development and evolving programs. 

**Key Responsibilities**

- 

Design and implement Evitable’s communication strategy from the ground up.

- 

Build and manage strong press and media relations, securing coverage that positions Evitable as an authoritative leader in the space.

- 

Map out the ecosystem of key journalists, organizations, movements, and potential partners.

- 

Coordinate production of prominent Evitable outputs, including video and writing.

- 

Advise on, draft messaging for, and provide speaker preparation for press, media, and public engagements.

- 

Stand up and scale digital engagement, building Evitable’s online presence across social platforms, testing and optimising content, and expanding its reach.

- 

Track relevant breaking and developing news stories; develop and implement effective responses. 

- 

Rapid-turnaround requests (op-eds, press materials, urgent media inquiries).

- 

Contribute assertively to strategic discussions, assisting the CEO to make final decisions.

- 

Editing written work such as blog posts.
