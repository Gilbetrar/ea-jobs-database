# Political Adviser, Food Systems

Eurogroup for Animals is the pan-European animal protection organisation which unites the voices of leading national and international organisations. We work to improve the welfare of as many animals as possible by leading and delivering coordinated advocacy and campaigns at European level. Eurogroup for Animals uses its knowledge and expertise to challenge EU decision makers and bring about positive change. By uniting our members and working together we magnify national activities to get our message across at European level. Eurogroup for Animals is a founding member of the World Federation for Animals (WFA), our global sister organisation. 

Eurogroup for Animals’ farm animal programme aims at transformative food system change by significantly reducing animal protein production and consumption. We do this by enhancing the uptake of plant-based diets and cellular agriculture products in tandem with driving systemic change in animal farming as well as by halting the development of new intensive farming systems, such as insect farming for feed.

#### **Purpose of the role**

As **Political Adviser - Food Systems**, you will implement the Brussels-based activities of our Food System work, carry out advocacy activities towards EU institutions on new intensive farming systems and lobby on related files in partnership with other political advisers and programme leaders. Additional responsibilities include scoping possible advocacy tactics and partnerships.

#### **Primary responsibilities**

- Conduct Eurogroup for Animals advocacy work on food systems, in particular insect farming for feed, connected to upcoming legislative files;
- Provide institutional stakeholders with scientific advice and policy solutions;
- Ensure close working relationships with the farm animals and political teams.

#### **Person specification**

- **A degree in political science, law or similar, or at least 5 years of experience of EU public affairs, **

- Experience with political or campaigning advocacy work and/or collaborations among civil society groups;
- Excellent communication skills;
- Fluent in English (spoken and written);
