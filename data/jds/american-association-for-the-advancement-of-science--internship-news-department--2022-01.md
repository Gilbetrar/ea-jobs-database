* In this role, you'll report on scientific issues for Science's news department as part of a program training journalists from underrepresented communities.
* Write articles for Science's daily online news site, weekly print magazine, and digital media platforms.
* Cover science-related events in Washington D.C., including press conferences, congressional hearings, and conferences.
* Collaborate with editors and reporters to learn multiple facets of science journalism.
* Communicate complex scientific topics to general audiences, expanding access to science journalism.
