#### Strategy Fellow

**About FRI**

Forecasting Research Institute (FRI) advances the science of forecasting to improve decision-making on high-stakes issues. Building on the work of Chief Scientist Philip Tetlock, FRI develops practical forecasting tools and applies them to society’s most critical decisions.

Our work includes:

- 

Creating panels of experts and skilled forecasters to make predictions about complex, long-term challenges facing society, including [AI progress](https://leap.forecastingresearch.org/), [bioweapons](https://forecastingresearch.org/ai-enabled-biorisk), and [nuclear risk](https://forecastingresearch.org/nuclear-risk).

- 

Methodological innovations for predicting hard-to-forecast outcomes, such as [applying adversarial collaboration to debates about the future of AI](https://forecastingresearch.org/ai-adversarial-collaboration) and [improving forecasts of low-probability events](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5025990).

- 

Using AI tools to enhance forecasting research. For example, we maintain a benchmark, [ForecastBench](https://www.forecastbench.org/), for evaluating the accuracy of AI-produced forecasts.

Our work has been featured in major news outlets, including [The Economist](https://www.economist.com/briefing/2025/07/24/ai-labs-all-or-nothing-race-leaves-no-time-to-fuss-about-safety), [TIME](https://time.com/7298645/ai-pandemic-5-times-more-likely/), and [Vox](https://www.vox.com/future-perfect/460222/ai-forecasting-tournament-superforecaster-expert-tetlock). Our team has presented our findings at side events at the United Nations, to Los Alamos National Laboratory, and to leading frontier AI companies, among other top decision-makers.

**The role**

We are hiring for Strategy Fellows who would work closely with senior leadership at FRI to push ahead work on high-priority organizational goals. This would involve doing a mix of strategic, research, and project management-oriented work.

Example tasks could include:

- 

Writing blog posts to contextualize our key findings, describing their relationship to other research findings and their implications for policy choices. For example, this could involve taking a result from our Longitudinal Expert AI Panel (LEAP) and writing a post about how it fits into the current AI policy discussion.

- 

Mapping the key policymakers, journalists, and other actors in a space (e.g., AI-biorisk, nuclear risk) to understand where and how our research results can be useful to decision makers.

- 

Conducting interviews with key actors we’re aiming to influence with our research to understand how our results are being used and how we can alter our approach to create more useful outputs.

- 

Identifying funding opportunities relevant to our work and helping to draft fundraising applications.

- 

Helping the senior leadership team with developing and tracking organizational goals.

**Qualifications**

The ideal candidate would have the following characteristics:

- 

Passionate about FRI’s mission

- 

Interested in a combination of organizational strategy, research, and fundraising

- 

Clear, explicit communicator who proactively checks in and drives work ahead independently where appropriate

- 

Strong writing skills

- 

Collaborative and friendly teammate

We believe that people with a wide range of experience levels could be a great fit for this role. We are open to talented generalists who are relatively early in their careers for this position.

#### The fine print

**Role details**

- 

We prefer full-time but are open to exceptional candidates who want to work at least 20 hours per week.

- 

This role is entirely remote, though would ideally involve travel 3-5 times per year to meet with other members of the team. Applicants must consistently be available between our core team meeting hours of 11am and 3pm ET.

- 

Compensation for this role will vary depending on experience, but we expect it to be between $80,000-$130,000.

- 

We provide 80% contribution towards fully funded health insurance for staff, 30 days per year of paid time off (including holidays), and up to 10 days of paid sick leave, among other benefits.

**Application process**

We are reviewing applications on a rolling basis and will take down this job posting when the role is filled.

The application process will vary by candidate but for successful candidates will typically involve:

- 

An initial application, including a 1-hour work test

- 

A paid 5-10 hour writing-oriented work trial

- 

Three to four virtual interviews with members of our team
