* In this role, you'll coordinate the implementation of humane electrical stunning systems in shrimp farms across Asia to improve welfare for billions of animals.
* Organize and supervise stunner installations and trainings for farm staff at shrimp production facilities.
* Coordinate with manufacturers on equipment logistics, shipping, and technical improvements.
* Collect data on humane slaughter implementation to support monitoring and evaluation efforts.
* Train farm personnel on humane slaughter techniques and proper equipment operation.
