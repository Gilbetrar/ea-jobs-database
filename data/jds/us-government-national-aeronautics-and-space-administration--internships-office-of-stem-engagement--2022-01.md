* These internships allow high school and college-level students to contribute to the agency’s mission to advance science, technology, aeronautics, and space exploration.
* Gain practical work experience while working side-by-side with mentors who are research scientists, engineers, and various professionals.
* Participate in three sessions annually to build a resume and strengthen career readiness.
* Engage in diverse tasks including procurement, budgeting, accounting, IT, and security support.
