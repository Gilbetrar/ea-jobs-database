# Get Involved
 

Are you excited to join [our mission](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/about-us/) and work with us on figuring out how to best reduce suffering?
 

**We would love to hear from you! Please register your interest [here](https://forms.gle/HLZjfPmySXoSZueQ9).**
 

We are keen to work with people who:
 
 
- are driven by maximising altruistic impact, and are intellectually curious about how to best do so 
- are passionate about our mission to reduce suffering 
- excel at self-directed work without strong supervision 
- are familiar with the core ideas of effective altruism, s-risks, and suffering-focused ethics 
 

As a small and entirely remote-based organisation, CRS is flexible about different forms of involvement. Depending on your needs, skills, and interests, we would consider either full employment, part-time work, a (paid) internship or work trial, informal research collaborations, or financial support for independent work. 
 

If you have any questions, please [contact us](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/contact/).
 
# Why work at CRS?
 

When working at CRS, you will:
 
 
- enjoy the intellectual freedom to explore the most important questions relating to how to best reduce suffering 
- work towards the shared goal of reducing suffering with highly driven, intellectually curious and caring colleagues 
- receive mentorship and feedback from people with many years of experience in suffering-focused prioritisation and macrostrategy 
- get the necessary financial and operational support that allows you to focus on maximising your impact 
- have the opportunity to shape the priorities of suffering reducers worldwide, as well the broader effective altruism community, through your research 
 

We believe that our open-ended research program on how to best reduce suffering fills an important gap. For more details on who we are, what we do, and why we do what we do, see the [About Us](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/about-us/) page, our [Strategic Plan](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/strategic-plan/), and our [Open Research Questions](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/open-research-questions/).
 

You can stay in touch, and learn about new opportunities at CRS, by [signing up for our newsletter](https://centerforreducingsuffering.org/newsletter).
