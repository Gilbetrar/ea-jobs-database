* In this role, you'll lead LEEP's paint program in Türkiye to eliminate childhood lead poisoning.
* Work with government officials to develop lead paint regulations and conduct stakeholder workshops.
* Engage with paint manufacturers to promote lead-free paint adoption and provide technical assistance.
* Conduct paint studies and create reports to support evidence-based regulatory decisions.
* Develop strategic plans, build local partnerships, and troubleshoot program challenges.
