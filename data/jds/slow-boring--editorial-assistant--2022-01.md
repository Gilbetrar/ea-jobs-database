Slow Boring is hiring! We’re sad to announce that our editorial assistant Ben Krauss will be moving on (to exciting things we’ll let him tell you about) this summer, and we’re looking for our next SB team member.

If you’re interested in joining us, more information and instructions on how to apply are below. If you know someone who might be interested, please feel free to share this post.

---

#### **Position Overview**

This is a full-time, primarily in-person position based in our downtown Washington, DC office, with some remote work possible. Start date: August 1, 2025 (flexible)

#### **Responsibilities**

- 

Copy edit and schedule newsletters

- 

Moderate community comment sections

- 

Create charts and graphics to accompany articles

- 

Research potential newsletter topics

- 

Draft and schedule podcast show notes and episode releases

- 

Respond to customer service inquiries

- 

Attend and document editorial meetings

- 

Provide general administrative support

#### **Qualifications**

- 

Demonstrated interest in and knowledge of American politics and policy issues

- 

Experience copy editing

- 

Strong attention to detail

- 

Ability to work independently and meet deadlines

- 

Familiarity with Slow Boring’s work

#### **Location & Schedule**

- 

Downtown Washington, DC 

- 

Flexible schedule with partial remote work possible

- 

In-person attendance required for meetings and certain work functions

#### **Compensation & Benefits**

- 

$55,000 annual salary

- 

Flexible leave policies

- 

Health insurance stipend available

#### **Notes**

- 

This is primarily a support role, not a writing position. Published work is not guaranteed, but you'll have opportunities to pitch ideas and will receive guidance and feedback on your work if you’re interested in developing your writing skills.

- 

Slow Boring is a small, family-run organization. While we can't offer extensive internal advancement opportunities, we're committed to investing in your professional development and preparing you for the next step in your career.

#### **How to Apply**

Email [jobs@slowboring.com](mailto:jobs@slowboring.com) with your resume and cover letter. You may include up to two writing samples, but this is not required.

#### **Additional Opportunity**

Want to work with us, but feel like this isn’t the right job for you? More experienced professionals familiar with our work are welcome to email us with a pitch for higher-level positions that align with your skills and experience.
