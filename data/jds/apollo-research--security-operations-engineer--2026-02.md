THE OPPORTUNITY

We're looking for a Security Operations Engineer to build operational security at Apollo Research from the ground up. 

Security at Apollo exists to maintain the trust of our frontier AI lab partners and enable our research mission. We're looking for someone who can execute fast and make pragmatic tradeoffs. You'll thrive in this role if you're service-oriented and trusted by non-security people; have deep hands-on experience with modern security tooling like MDM, EDR, and identity management; and can own a broad set of responsibilities.

This role sits within our Operations team and reports to the COO. You'll work closely with our CISO, who handles certification, threat modelling, and stakeholder relationships with AI labs; and our engineering team, who own infrastructure-as-code and secure software architecture.

### Responsibilities

  - **Own the deployment and operation of Apollo's security tooling.** Select, implement, and manage controls including EDR/MDR, SIEM, vulnerability management, Kandji (MDM), and email protection. Establish backup and disaster recovery processes.

  - **Define and enforce policies for how Apollo uses AI tools**. Manage which models and tools we access, how API keys are provisioned and rotated, and what data can be sent where. Balance security requirements with researcher productivity.
  - **Champion Apollo’s security culture. **Champion Apollo's security culture. Build buy-in for security practices by helping people understand the 'why' behind policies. Be the trusted point of contact for security questions, run training and phishing exercises, and support incident response.
  - **Support Apollo's compliance efforts. **Work with the CISO to implement required controls for ISO 27001, SOC 2, and additional frameworks, in order to maintain the trust of our frontier AI lab partners.

ABOUT APOLLO 

The capabilities of current AI systems are evolving at a rapid pace. While these advancements offer tremendous opportunities, they also present significant risks, such as the potential for deliberate misuse or the deployment of sophisticated yet misaligned models. At [Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/), our primary concern lies with deceptive alignment, a phenomenon where a model appears to be aligned but is, in fact, misaligned and capable of evading human oversight.

We work on the detection of scheming (e.g. building evaluations), the science of scheming (e.g. model organisms), and scheming mitigations (e.g. anti-scheming and control). We closely work with multiple frontier AI companies, e.g. to test their models before deployment or collaborate on scheming mitigations.

At Apollo, we aim for a culture that emphasizes truth-seeking, being goal-oriented, giving and receiving constructive feedback, and being friendly and helpful.
