* In this role, you'll conduct research to inform animal advocacy strategy by analysing policy reforms and their impact on animals.
* Analyse corporate and public policy reforms to assess their potential impact on animals' welfare and lives.
* Prepare detailed reports, policy briefs, and white papers on animal protection policy issues.
* Conduct interviews with experts and stakeholders to gather evidence and insights.
* Synthesise information from diverse research sources into concise, decision-relevant summaries.
