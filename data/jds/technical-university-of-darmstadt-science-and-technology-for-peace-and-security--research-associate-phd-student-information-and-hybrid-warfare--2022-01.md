# Research Associate / PhD Student (m/w/d)
 

Initially for a period of 3 years with the option for extension. Offered as a 100% position in [pay group 13 of the TU Darmstadt collective agreement (TV-TUDa)](https://www.personalrat.tu-darmstadt.de/personalrat_aktuelles/a_z_pr/a-z_details_238272.de.jsp).
 
### About our Group
 

The *Science and Technology for Peace and Security (PEASEC)* research group, led by Prof. Dr. Dr. Christian Reuter, at the Technical University of Darmstadt integrates **computer science** with **peace and security research**. We focus on creating innovative approaches in **cybersecurity and privacy**, **human-computer interaction**, and **peace and conflict studies**.
 

At PEASEC, we are committed to addressing real-world challenges through interdisciplinary collaboration and research.
 
 
- **Interdisciplinary collaboration**: Work with a team that combines expertise from diverse fields to solve pressing societal issues. 
- **Recognized research**: Our work has been acknowledged at leading conferences, including recent Best Paper Awards (CHI, CSCW, ISCRAM 2024). 
- **Real-world impact**: Contribute to projects that connect cutting-edge research with practical solutions for peace and security. 
 
### Your Tasks
 

As part of your role, you will pursue a PhD at the intersection of **Cybersecurity and Privacy**, **Human-Computer Interaction **and **Peace and Conflict Research**. Your research will focus on one or more of the following areas:
 
 
- **Peace Informatics and Technical Peace Research**: Explore topics such as hybrid warfare, the impact of technology on war and arms control. 
- **Crisis Informatics and Information Warfare**: Investigate the role of social media and collaborative technologies in conflict and crisis situations, including opinion manipulation and disinformation. 
- **Usable Safety, Security, and Privacy**: Contribute to the development of security and privacy-enhancing technologies that are accessible and user-friendly. 
 

We offer:
 
 
- **Comprehensive academic support**: Guidance throughout your PhD journey, from planning and publishing research to drafting your dissertation. 
- **State-of-the-art resources**: Access to excellent technical equipment to facilitate your work. 
- **Development opportunities**: Attend international conferences and benefit from tailored opportunities for personal and professional growth. 
 

Join us to make a meaningful contribution to interdisciplinary research at the forefront of technology and societal impact!
 
### Your Profile
 

We are looking for candidates who meet the following qualifications and bring enthusiasm for interdisciplinary research:
 
 
- **Academic Background**: 
 
  - Successfully completed graduate studies (with good or very good results) in **Computer Science**, **Cybersecurity**, **Human-Computer Interaction**, or related fields. 
  - Other relevant study areas include **Peace and Conflict Research** with a technical focus, **Psychology**, **Data and Discourse Studies**, or **Cognitive Science**. 
  
- **Motivation and Research Interest**: 
 
  - A strong intrinsic motivation to engage in high-quality and impactful research. 
  - A clear ambition to pursue independent research and work towards a PhD. 
  
- **Personal Skills**: 
 
  - A collaborative mindset, determination, and a proactive approach to challenges. 
  - Proficiency in **English** (very good level). German language skills would be appreciated but are not mandatory. 
  
 

If you are eager to contribute to cutting-edge research at the intersection of technology and societal challenges, we look forward to your application!

We look forward to receiving your application! Please send your complete application as a **single PDF document** via email to **Prof. Dr. Dr. Christian Reuter** ([jobs@peasec.de]()). If applicable, please also refer to the official job posting in our **job portal**. Your application should include the following documents:
 
 
- **Letter of motivation** (max. 800 words):
 Outline your research ideas and methods, explain why you are a good fit for this position, and describe your interest in the research topics. 
- **Curriculum Vitae**:
 Include language, methodological, and IT skills, as well as any relevant publications – and if you like your photo 
- **Certificates or an overview of grades**:
 Provide documentation of your academic achievements (e.g., Abitur, Bachelor, Master). 
- **Research/writing samples**:
 Submit examples of your academic work, such as scientific papers, Bachelor’s or Master’s thesis or paper
