* Identify and build relationships with key stakeholders across government, academia, and NGO sectors.
* Conduct a desk review of Rwanda's "lead landscape," analyzing health impacts and existing policies.
* Research operational requirements for Pure Earth to establish presence in Rwanda.
* Develop a project work plan aligned with local context and organizational mission.
