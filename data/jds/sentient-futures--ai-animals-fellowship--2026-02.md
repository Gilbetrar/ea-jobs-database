# 🤖 AI×Animals
 
---
 

[AI×Animals](https://sentient-futures.notion.site/AI-Animals-25071f3c4caf8057a721e0f23844b9f6) surveys the impact of current and emerging AI technologies on animal welfare. Fellows will engage with high-stakes issues such as precision livestock farming, the use of machine learning to "decode" animal communication, genetic engineering to reduce animal suffering, and the intersection between animal advocacy and AI risks. This program will equip you with the knowledge, skills, and connections to steer AI development and deployment towards animal-positive outcomes.
