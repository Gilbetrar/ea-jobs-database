* The Digital Media Accelerator supports creators producing content that raises awareness about AI developments and issues, particularly regarding AI safety and risk.
* Potential projects include YouTube explainer videos about AI safety topics, TikTok accounts dedicated to daily AI news, newsletters explaining AI safety research papers, or podcast series on AGI progress and implications.
* Support is available for creators who already have a following and are interested in AI safety awareness, or those with compelling ideas for impactful content.
