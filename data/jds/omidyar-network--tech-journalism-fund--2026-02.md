* The Tech Journalism Fund provides project funding from $5,000 to $25,000 to support reporting costs for journalists working on a singular, time and resource intensive reporting project.
* Costs covered may include funding for travel, research and fact-checking support, multimedia enhancement, access to data, and stipends.
* Potential projects include topics related to how technology and society intersect.
* Priority areas include legislation on regulating AI, investigative pieces on the AI industry, AI's impact on families, and how workers are contending with technological advances.
