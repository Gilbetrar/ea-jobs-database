## How the fellowships work

Over seven months, you’ll commit fulltime to a career dedicated to making the greatest possible impact.

### Selection

Our application process focuses on skills over credentials. We test what matters by aiming to identify talent based on the skills crucial for success in each role. Applications take place over a number of rounds, over the course of about a month. The final step is matching fellows with host organizations, which will happen in the last weeks of June.

1st month
### Training

The fellowships begin with a month of intensive training, including a week at a unique location. The program builds key skills for driving change, with a special focus on how to effectively influence policy.

month 2 - month 7

### Placement

During the selection process, you’ll be carefully matched with a host organization, where you’ll spend most of the program. These strategically chosen organizations offer immediate opportunities to make an impact while providing six months of valuable, hands-on experience.

month 2 - month 7
### peer-learning

During your placement, the fellowship group meets regularly to collaborate, share insights, and learn from each other’s experiences.

### Next steps

We provide external career coaching, network sharing, public speaking opportunities, fundraising training, and more to position you for a long-term career in your cause area.

## Should you apply?

### Commitment

Fellows quit their job and commit full-time for the duration of the program, dedicating months to impactful work. Participants receive a modest but reasonable monthly salary to cover the cost of living and support their transition into a high-impact career.

### Experience

We seek candidates with approximately ten years of experience (minimum five), including professionals from law, consulting, lobbying, economics, policy, research, taxation, communications, and marketing. As this is a career change program, no prior experience in the topic you choose is required.

### Practical

Our programs are open to applicants from around the globe — be sure to check the specifics for each fellowship. This includes an in-person week of training, but are otherwise largely remote. Some host organizations may require a willingness to temporarily relocate for the duration of the program.

# Frequently Asked Questions

 

What criteria are used to select candidates for the Fellowships?

The selection process is skill-based, focusing on the relevant skills crucial for success in the role rather than just academic credentials. After the initial application, the process includes a three-hour skill test, a 45 minute online interview focusing on case scenarios and personal fit, and a final one-hour interview with a potential host organisation.

What are Moral Ambition Fellowships and what purpose do they serve?

Moral Ambition Fellowships are an opportunity to radically transform your career and be prepared to contribute at the highest level to urgent global issues. The fellowship is a seven-month program, including a one-month intensive training that sharpens you into an effective advocate for positive change, followed by a six-month placement at a leading NGO or think tank working in the field of a Tobacco Free Future or Food Transition.

How and when can I apply for a Moral Ambition Fellowship?

You can apply for the second edition of the EU fellowship during the application round from April 2 to May 12, 2025. The first round of application will take approximately 1.5 hours.

What are the expected outcomes for fellows by the end of the program?

By the end of the program, fellows are expected to have developed comprehensive skills in advocacy, made substantial contributions to their field, and prepared a foundation for a career focused on making a difference in global issues related to their fellowship topic area.

How does the Fellowship program ensure diversity and inclusion among the Fellows?

During our selection process we focus on skills instead of traditional credentials.The School for Moral Ambition embraces diversity and inclusion, welcoming applicants of all races, religions, gender identities, sexual orientations, ages, national origins, abilities, and other diverse backgrounds.
