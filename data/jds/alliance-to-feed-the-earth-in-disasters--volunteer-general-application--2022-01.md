* In this role, volunteers contribute significantly to ALLFED's research, operations, communications, and preparedness.
* Play an active role in research, participating in collaborative meetings and aiming to publish peer-reviewed articles.
* Assist in maintaining and improving ALLFED's organisational systems.
* Work on communications projects, including social media presence, content editing and writing, and website improvement initiatives.
* Develop real-world plans for preparedness and learn about interdisciplinary action through collaboration with various ALLFED teams.
