### About Constellation
 

Constellation is an independent research center that brings together people throughout the AI safety ecosystem to accelerate insight, research, and talent through better cooperation. Unlike a conference or summit, Constellation operates continuously, in the form of a physical workspace as well as conference-style talks, workshops, and training bootcamps. Continuous operation allows for relationships and conversations to develop over time, meaningfully fostering trust, collaboration, and shared insight. It also makes Constellation a natural field-building hub by rapidly inspiring, orienting, and connecting people who are newer to the field.
 

Based in Berkeley, CA, our shared workspace hosts over 250 people per week across dozens of AI safety organizations in nonprofits, academia, industry, and government. Hundreds of other researchers spend time at Constellation for shorter visits each year. We believe this is the strongest and highest-output network of AI safety researchers in the world; dozens of participants in past Constellation programs have gone on to safety-focused roles at companies such as METR, Redwood Research, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, OpenAI, and the US and UK Safety Institutes.
 
### About this Role
 

As a Program Coordinator on the Convening Team, you will play a critical, full-time role focused on measuring and improving our program effectiveness and end-to-end user experience. This position combines rigorous data analysis with hands-on community and event support. You will be responsible for conducting metrics and impact analysis, performing user research to understand community needs, and enhancing the daily experience of our members. You will also work on strategic analysis and assist with planning and running high-stakes events.
 
#### Key Responsibilities
 

**User Research, User Experience, and Strategic Analysis**
 
 
- Conduct user interviews and quantitative data collection to deeply understand the needs of researchers, their organizations, and what enables their most impactful work. 
- Conduct metrics and impact analysis to measure program effectiveness. Analyze quantitative data to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement. 
- Analyze our end-to-end user experience and make recommendations on improvements to make based on your strategic analysis. 
 

**Community Experience, Design, and Implementation**
 
 
- Design and continuously improve the end-to-end user experience (UX), including onboarding, visitor experience, stocking, slack engagement and design, office utilization and design, and the general social environment. 
- Provide strategic problem-solving and rapid response to emerging community needs, using triage frameworks to prioritize interventions by impact and urgency. 
- Address community needs through reactive work as it arises. 
 

**Hosting & Membership **
 
 
- Investigate and evaluate who to admit as members into our space, including strategic and theory of change analysis. 
 

**Events & Communications**
 
 
- Assist with the planning and execution of events, such as elite technical events for frontier AI company employees and educational events for policymakers in DC 
- Manage website updates and external communications, including creating content that showcases our programs and ensures our public presence accurately reflects our work and impact 
 

*The specific balance of time spent across these responsibility areas will vary based on organizational priorities and program needs. Additional related duties may include supporting other areas of program operations, contributing to program strategy development, and assisting with special projects.*
