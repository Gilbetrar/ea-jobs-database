ABOUT APOLLO

[Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/) is a rapidly growing AI safety and model evaluation company, providing third-party evaluations for frontier AI models. The capabilities of current AI systems are evolving rapidly. While these advancements offer tremendous opportunities, they also present significant risks, such as the potential for deliberate misuse or the deployment of sophisticated yet misaligned models. At Apollo Research, our primary concern lies with deceptive alignment, a phenomenon where a model appears aligned but is, in fact, misaligned and capable of evading human oversight.

At Apollo, we aim for a culture that emphasizes truth-seeking, being goal-oriented, giving and receiving constructive feedback, and being friendly and helpful. If you’re interested in more details about what it’s like working at Apollo, you can find more information [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cMyxLD24Llggd0n7cGMdJt6PPkdpuHQtHw8IqddeS6Y/edit?usp=sharing).

ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY

This is Apollo's first full-time legal hire. You'll build and lead our legal and compliance functions from the ground up, establishing scalable systems and processes across UK, US, and select EU jurisdictions. The role combines setting high-level strategic direction with hands-on execution, spanning commercial contracting, corporate legal, employment/HR, IP protection, statutory reporting, fundraising support, and compliance management (75% operational expansion; 25% compliance oversight). You'll report to the COO daily and have the potential to grow into a General Counsel role as the legal group expands. Ideal candidates may come from in-house legal at AI and tech start-ups, or lawyers at Big Law or large technology companies who are seeking greater ownership and impact. You will design and own Apollo’s legal function from first principles.

### KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  - **First in-house legal leadership**
  - Serve as Apollo’s first in-house legal hire, owning day-to-day legal execution while laying the foundations for a future legal team.
  - **Commercial and fundraising legal foundations (primary focus)**
  - Draft, review, and negotiate a range of commercial agreements (MSAs, SOWs, ToS, etc.) with customers, vendors, funders, and partners, including Frontier Labs and large enterprises.
  - Support the legal aspects of launching and scaling Apollo’s product offerings, including customer terms, evaluation agreements. DPAs, partner agreements, and risk allocation appropriate for enterprise customers.
  - Review and finalize legal documentation for equity financings (including venture capital investments) and grant agreements, in collaboration with internal stakeholders.
  - **Long-term legal strategy and mission-aligned governance**
  - Define and own Apollo’s long-term legal strategy, with a forward-looking horizon (2+ years), aligned to company growth, product roadmap, and mission protection.
  - Build the frameworks, playbooks, and operating model needed to execute that strategy as Apollo scales.
  - Support the COO on corporate governance and mission protection, including charter and bylaw design, internal policies, and governance mechanisms that safeguard Apollo’s mission as the organization scales.
  - Help advise on governance, control, and decision-rights frameworks appropriate for a venture-backed public benefit company, balancing growth, investor expectations, and mission protection.
  - **Intellectual property and brand protection**
  - Protect Apollo’s brand and intellectual property by filing and maintaining patents or trademarks, and supporting enforcement actions in the UK and US.
  - **International expansion and cross-border operations**
  - Support the creation and maintenance of legal entities and operating subsidiaries in new jurisdictions.
  - Lead legal work for cross-border employment and immigration matters, including maintaining Apollo’s UK sponsorship capabilities and assisting with U.S. immigration processes where applicable.
  - Review, negotiate, and coordinate the legal aspects of office leases and related real-estate documentation across the UK, US, and Switzerland.
  - **Compliance, risk management, and legal operations**
  - Build compliance and legal risk management systems and processes that keep Apollo operating legally across all jurisdictions.
  - Maintain lean, efficient legal and compliance processes that support rapid scaling without unnecessary bureaucracy.
  - Continue to build Apollo’s legal knowledge base, templates, and playbooks.
  - Manage and strengthen relationships with external counsel to ensure high-quality, cost-efficient legal support, and identify opportunities to expand or optimize Apollo’s legal provider network.

### KEY REQUIREMENTS

  - 6-8 PQE (Post-Qualification Experience) as a mid- or senior-level associate at a law firm, as an in-house legal counsel (preferably at a large technology company), or as a combination of both. 
  - Qualified to practice law in at least one common-law jurisdiction; UK or US qualification strongly preferred.
  - Operate at high velocity in a lean, high-growth environment, shifting seamlessly between strategic and hands-on legal work.
  - Operate as legal problem solver. 
  - Strong experience drafting and reviewing commercial contracts and leading negotiations through to final agreement
  - Demonstrated ability to negotiate assertively and strategically with a wide range of counterparties (e.g., enterprises, labs, funders, vendors)
  - Experience handling corporate legal matters across mult. jurisdictions, such as statutory filings, basic governance documentation, and entity maintenance
  - Think several steps ahead to identify emerging risks and design legal and compliance systems for where Apollo will be, not just where we are today
  - Experience working with external counsels
  - Deep interest in AI safety and in supporting a mission-driven organization
