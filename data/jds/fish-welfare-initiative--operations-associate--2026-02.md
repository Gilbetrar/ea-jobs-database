## About Fish Welfare Initiative 
 

Fish Welfare Initiative (FWI) is a nonprofit animal welfare organization dedicated to improving the welfare of farmed fishes in Asia. In India, we do this primarily through our main program, the [Alliance for Responsible Aquaculture](http://fwi.fish/ara), which focuses on implementing welfare interventions such as improved water quality and reduced stocking densities. We also conduct [research](https://www.fishwelfareinitiative.org/blog/tags/r-d) to explore new interventions and build the evidence base needed to help more fishes in the future.

## How You Will Make a Difference
 

The Operations team ensures that our programs, people, and finances are supported by reliable processes and clear internal structures.
 

 
 

As Operations Associate, you’ll take ownership of day-to-day operational systems, while supporting the Operations Manager on others. Your core focus areas will include finance, admin, documentation, and recruitment and hiring processes, with opportunities to take on ownership of additional operational systems and projects as you grow in the role.
 

​
 
## Key Responsibilities
 

In this role, you’ll support the smooth running of FWI’s operations through the following responsibilities:

Maintain and execute day-to-day operational systems (finance, admin, and documentation)
  
-  

Support hiring processes, including recruitment support, and early-stage screening
  
-  

Keep trackers, files, and internal documentation accurate and up to date
  
-  

Assist with process improvements and take on additional operational projects over time
