###  About the engagement 

The Nucleic Acid Observatory (NAO) is scaling a city‑wide swab‑sampling initiative to strengthen early detection of emerging pathogens. We're always looking for motivated individuals to support this mission. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and we'll reach out if you seem like a good fit.

As a Field Sampler, you’ll spend one to four days a week approaching members of the public, inviting them to participate in our study, and handing samples off for analysis. You’ll be paid on a per-hour basis, with an additional bonus depending on the number of swabs collected. Given past sampling rates, we project an hourly wage of $22-$34.
 
 
###  Responsibilities 

######  What you'll do 

- Recruit participants who will self-collect swab samples at various sites around Boston on two to four weekdays from 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM (including a paid 30-minute lunch break).
- Engage and reassure participants: answering questions and clearly explaining our work.
- Handle samples responsibly, following our simple biosafety checklist.
- Represent the NAO with professionalism and enthusiasm.

######  What a typical workday looks like: 

- 09:15 – 09:30: Meet your sampling partner at 1 Broadway (Kendall Sq.). Pick up sampling materials and travel to Boston.
- 09:30 – 15:00: Sample collection (including a paid 30-minute lunch break)
- 15:00 – 15:30: Return gear to 1 Broadway, log hours & sampling metrics. 
 
 
###  Who we're looking for 

- Extraversion: You’re comfortable approaching strangers and enjoy friendly conversation.
- Caring for the NAO’s mission: You are motivated to help us in our mission to better detect new pathogen outbreaks.
- Reliable & conscientious: You show up on time, follow protocols, and log accurate data.
- Availability: You can start work immediately.
