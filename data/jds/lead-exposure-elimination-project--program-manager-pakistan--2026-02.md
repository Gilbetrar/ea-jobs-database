* In this role, you'll lead LEEP's spices programs in Pakistan aimed at eliminating childhood lead poisoning.
* Work with government officials to introduce and implement regulations for lead-free spices.
* Engage with spice wholesalers and retailers to eliminate lead adulteration from their products.
* Conduct research studies, collect samples, and prepare reports on lead content in spices.
* Design project plans, recruit local consultants, and build partnerships to achieve LEEP's lead elimination goals.
