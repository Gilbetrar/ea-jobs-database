# The SandboxAQ Residency Program

#### Accelerating the Future of AI with the Next Generation of Innovators

The SandboxAQ Residency Program cultivates the next generation of leaders, empowering early-career scientists, engineers, and innovators to tackle society's hardest challenges. Our residents, interns, and postdocs dive deep into transformative projects driven by Large Quantitative Models (LQMs).
‍
Open to STEM graduate students, medical students, MBA candidates, undergraduates, and postdocs, the program brings together a global network of emerging talent and seasoned experts in a highly collaborative environment where unique perspectives drive impact. Participants receive mentorship and entry into an expansive, lifelong professional network. This is a space for ambitious ideas to take shape.

[](#)

Our Residency Program welcomes top candidates from diverse fields, including:

**AI · PHYSICS · CHEMISTRY · BIOLOGY · NEUROSCIENCE · COMPUTER SCIENCE · CYBERSECURITY · MATHEMATICS · ENGINEERING · DRUG DESIGN · FINANCIAL ENGINEERING · MEDICINE · BUSINESS**
