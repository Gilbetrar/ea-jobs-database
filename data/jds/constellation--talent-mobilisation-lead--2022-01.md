### About Constellation
 

The AI safety field lacks the capacity, capability, and cooperation needed to safely navigate transformative AI. Constellation addresses these gaps, by **mobilizing** new and existing talent (to improve field **capacity**), **incubating** and **growing** projects, teams and organizations (to improve field **capability**), and **convening** field contributors (to improve field **cooperation)**. 
 

While our overall mission requires work across all the necessary areas needed to address risks from transformative AI, Constellation is **especially well positioned to support research**, because of our established track record of hosting and collaborating with research organizations. We support research at multiple levels: we [host](https://www.constellation.org/programs?hsLang=en#:~:text=General%20Hosting%20and%20Visitors) and [incubate](https://www.constellation.org/programs/constellation-incubator?hsLang=en) research organisations, we support individual researchers via our [Residency](https://www.constellation.org/programs?hsLang=en), and we support cohorts of independent researchers as [Visiting Fellows](https://www.constellation.org/programs/visiting-fellows?hsLang=en) (and previously our [Astra research fellowship](https://www.constellation.org/programs/astra-fellowship?hsLang=en)), and by partnering with external research programs like [MATS](https://matsprogram.org/) and [Anthropic Fellows](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2024/anthropic-fellows-program/).
 

**But there’s so much more to do. **There is more talent that can contribute to safety than has been mobilized into the field, and the field as a whole needs to grow to meet the demand for top talent working across all areas. 
 

 
 
### About this role
 

The future of AI safety will be shaped by **who** works on **what**, and **when**. The right people, in the right roles, at the right moment can change the trajectory of the entire field. That’s where you come in. As **Talent Mobilization Lead**, you won’t just fill positions, you’ll help **shape the talent map** of the AI safety ecosystem. You’ll spot high-potential individuals before others do, help them navigate pivotal career moves, and connect them to roles where their work will have outsized impact.
 

This is a rare opportunity to operate at the intersection of strategy, networks, and execution, working with individual talent, leading research labs, and operational teams to make sure the most capable minds are deployed where they’re needed most. If you want your work to directly accelerate the progress of an entire mission-driven field, this is the role.
 

You’ll identify and engage promising individuals, guide them through key career transitions, and facilitate their movement into high-impact roles. You’ll work across organizational boundaries, partnering closely with internal program leads (e.g., Research Program Managers) and external stakeholders at frontier labs and aligned nonprofits.
 

**Your time is primarily split between:**
 
 
- **Sourcing and identifying talent** through networks, programs, and referrals. 
- **Placement and transition support** including matchmaking, advisory conversations, and warm handoffs to hiring organizations. 
 

Your work will directly accelerate the field’s progress by:
 
 
- Sourcing and preparing top talent to step into priority roles. 
- Guiding individuals through transitions e.g., from a fellowship into a frontier lab, from academia into applied AI safety research, or from an industry role into policy-focused work. 
- Strengthening the systems and partnerships that make talent deployment faster, more precise, and more effective over time. 
 

 
 
#### Key responsibilities:
 

**Strategic Talent Deployment**
 
 
- Proactively identify and engage promising individuals across technical, policy, operational, and interdisciplinary domains. 
- Assess fit for high-impact opportunities through vetting and triage conversations (e.g., helping determine whether someone is best suited for a research engineering role or a policy fellowship). 
- Match individuals to **priority-aligned roles** across the ecosystem, leveraging curated shortlists, targeted introductions, and structured placement processes. 
 

**Career Transitions & Advisory Support**
 
 
- Provide lightweight but high-value advisory support such as reviewing a candidate’s transition plan from a fellowship program to a frontier lab, or discussing trade-offs between roles at different organizations. 
- Develop and maintain resources to support career moves (e.g., curated job boards, pathway guides, or specialized Slack channels for active opportunities). 
- Coordinate warm handoffs to hiring organizations, program managers, or advisors who can offer domain-specific guidance. 
 

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**
 
 
- Partner closely with program leads, research managers, and operations staff to identify upcoming needs and facilitate placements from internal programs. 
- Build and maintain strong relationships with external field-building organizations to share intel, reduce duplication, and strengthen collective efforts. 
 

**Ecosystem Coordination & Field Intelligence**
 
 
- Track and analyze talent flows, hiring trends, and emerging gaps in the field; translate insights into actionable recommendations for leadership and program design. 
- Maintain a high-context understanding of organizational needs, leadership shifts, and strategic priorities across the AI safety space. 
 

**Infrastructure & Process Building**
 
 
- Manage tooling for talent tracking and matchmaking. 
- Improve and scale repeatable processes for high-quality introductions, placements, and follow-ups. 
- Contribute to the design, launch, and evaluation of new talent deployment initiatives. 
 
#### You may be a good fit if you:
 
 
- Have 6+ years of experience in talent development, recruiting, program management, or people operations, ideally in mission-driven, technical, or complex environments. 
- Are energized by connecting people to meaningful work and supporting them through career inflection points. 
- Bring strong interpersonal judgment and can hold high-trust, nuanced conversations with a wide range of stakeholders. 
- Have strong operational instincts—organized, systems-minded, and able to triage, follow up, and execute effectively. 
- Are familiar with the AI safety ecosystem (or similar mission-driven technical fields) and excited to contribute to its effectiveness and maturity.Are mission-aligned and values-driven, with a clear sense of why this work matters.
