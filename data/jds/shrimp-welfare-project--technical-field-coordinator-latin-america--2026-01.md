* In this role, you'll implement humane slaughter practices in shrimp farms across Latin America by coordinating electrical stunning systems.
* Organize installations and technical trainings for farm staff on shrimp stunners and humane slaughter methods.
* Coordinate manufacturing, shipping, and logistics between producers and equipment manufacturers.
* Collect implementation data and producer feedback to support monitoring and evaluation efforts.
* Engage with producers and seafood companies to secure commitments to humane slaughter practices.
