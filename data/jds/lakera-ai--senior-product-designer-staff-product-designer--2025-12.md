Our values

1
### Less but better

There are always ten things we could be doing at any time, but probably only one that really matters. We always want to work to identify what matters most and then make sure it stands out. This requires us to be confident in saying no to many things and eliminating unnecessary complexity and distractions. Quality over quantity.

2
### Take risks and learn

We always want to be learning. The best way to do that is to prioritize speed over making the correct decision and focus on getting quick feedback. We want to make good mistakes. We want to get better as individuals and as a company every single day.

3
### We are drivers, not passengers

We get satisfaction from making things happen. We feel a strong sense of ownership and demand high standards from ourselves and others. We value urgency, ambition, and boldness. 

4
### Execute as one

Collaboration is key to our success. We always strive to increase transparency and avoid silos. We realize that we rely on each other and hold each other accountable. When we disagree, we treat each other with respect and humility. We have zero tolerance for toxic and unprofessional behavior.

5
### In IT to win IT

It’s important to us to always raise the bar in whatever we are doing. Our customers come first. We commit to excellence. We play to win.

How do I know if Lakera Guard is right for my LLM use case?

Lakera Guard is compatible with any LLM, including popular ones like GPT-X, Claude, LLaMa, Gemini, or your custom models. It's ideal for a wide range of applications, such as customer support chatbots, internal Q&A systems, content or code generation tools, LLM plugins, and more.

Regardless of your specific use case, Lakera Guard can be seamlessly integrated to ensure secure deployment. For unique use cases, we encourage you to reach out to our team for more details at contact@lakera.ai.
