# Application Scorer

### Part-time contractor · ~2–3 hrs/week · Remote · 30 USD/hr
 

  ​
 

​
 

About Successif
 

Successif is a career advising program helping professionals transition into high-impact roles in AI safety and governance. We provide personalized coaching, specialized training, and consulting services.
 

 
 

The Role
 

We're looking for a part-time contractor to score incoming applications to our advising program. You'll use a structured rubric to evaluate each applicant's background, motivation, and fit — and flag edge cases for team discussion.
 

This is a focused, async-friendly role ideal for someone who wants to contribute meaningfully to AI safety talent pipelines without a heavy time commitment.
 

 
 

What you'll do
 
 
-  

Score applications against our rubric (~2–3 hours per week)
  
-  

Flag edge cases and dealbreakers with brief written notes
  
-  

Communicate asynchronously with team members to align on borderline cases
  
 

 
 

What success looks like
 
 
-  

Ensuring a quick turnaround time for all incoming applications.
  
-  

Maintaining high scoring consistency (inter-rater reliability).
  
 

​
 

What we're looking for
 
 
-  

Familiarity with the AI safety / AI governance space
  
-  

Sound judgment — can read between the lines and assess potential, not just credentials
  
-  

Clear, direct written communication
  
-  

Reliability and attention to detail
  
 

 
 

Nice to have
 
 
-  

Prior experience in hiring, admissions, or program selection
  
-  

Familiarity with high-impact career frameworks or global catastrophic risk reduction discourse
  
 

 
 

Compensation & logistics
 
 
-  

30 USD per hour, paid monthly. Fully remote and async. We'll invite shortlisted candidates to complete a work test before confirming the engagement. This is a contractor role, and the successful candidate will invoice Successif to be paid. 
  
-  

Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis, but we look to fill the role within the coming week if possible.
