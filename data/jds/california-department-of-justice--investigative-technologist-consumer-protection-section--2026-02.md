### Job Description and Duties
 

 

Investigative Technologists (Information Technology Specialist III) wanted to conduct technical investigations and provide subject matter expertise in consumer protection, data privacy and security, and technology investigations and enforcement work in the California Attorney General’s Office. 

The Consumer Protection Section of the California Attorney General’s Office and its Privacy Unit handle complex investigations and enforcement actions across various business sectors, often with technical aspects such as data privacy and security, artificial intelligence, children’s online safety, financial products and services, autonomous vehicles, computer-aided creation of contraband items, and other technological issues encountered in the enforcement of consumer protection and privacy laws. The Section’s Investigative Technologists will serve as integral members of interdisciplinary investigative and enforcement teams, working closely with the section’s attorneys, auditors, analysts, investigators, and other professionals to perform and support this work as experts in technology. They will also support the section’s legislative, policy, amicus, education, and other work by offering their technological expertise. They will also work to uphold the Office’s values of public service, fairness, equity, and protecting the public from technology-enabled harms. 

The Investigative Technologist will serve under the administrative direction of the Senior Assistant Attorney General, performing complex technical investigations and providing subject matter expertise in support of the Section’s consumer protection, privacy, and technology enforcement matters and initiatives, including investigations into potential violations of the Unfair Competition Law, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and other laws. 

If this role is of interest to you, we welcome you to apply to join the Consumer Protection team!

### Desirable Qualifications
 
 In addition to evaluating each candidate's relative ability, as demonstrated by quality and breadth of experience, the following factors will provide the basis for competitively evaluating each candidate:  
 

• Two or more years’ experience in executing or leading technology projects using skills in software engineering, cybersecurity, interface design, product management, data science, or research.

• Ability to apply a range of technical approaches, including data science, systems analysis, penetration testing, forensic data extraction, or human-centered design, to collect and analyze qualitative and quantitative data.

• Basic familiarity with relevant laws, regulations, and policies of federal and state, agencies, as well as federal and state legislation pertaining to privacy rights.

• Strategic thinking and problem-solving skills, including the ability to anticipate challenges and develop actionable solutions.

• Experience in mobile technology, cloud systems, digital tracking technologies, and artificial intelligence.

• Hands-on experience building, auditing, maintaining, or pressure-testing working systems.

• Excellent written and oral communication skills, including the ability to communicate and present complex technical topics to a non-technical audience.

 • Strong organizational and analytical skills with the ability to multi-task.

• Access to a professional work environment or workspace.
