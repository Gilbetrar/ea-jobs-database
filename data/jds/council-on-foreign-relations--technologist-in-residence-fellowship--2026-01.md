* In this role, you'll research issues at the intersection of emerging technology and foreign policy as a Council on Foreign Relations fellow.
* Produce policy-oriented analysis exploring how technological advancements impact national security.
* Engage with CFR experts and members to build professional networks across disciplines.
* Work on-site in New York or Washington DC during this 10-month, full-time fellowship.
* Develop a research proposal addressing challenges where technology and US foreign policy intersect.
