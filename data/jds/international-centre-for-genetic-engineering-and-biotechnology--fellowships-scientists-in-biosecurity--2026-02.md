* The ICGEB offers early-career scientists a fellowship at ICGEB laboratories to receive hands on training in life sciences and detection and response to biological threats.
* Topics include molecular detection tools of biological threats, epidemiology and genomic surveillance of pathogens, drug discovery, vaccine development, and more.
* The fellowship includes a stipend, exposure to advanced techniques and methodologies, opportunities for mentoring and complementary skills, integration into a world-class international scientific community, and training in biosafety and biosecurity.
