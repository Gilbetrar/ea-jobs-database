#### **Lawyer**
  

 

Position: Lawyer

Contract Term: 1 year fixed term (with the hope to extend), full time

Organisation: The Animal Law Foundation

Location: London

Salary: £32,000 to £45,000 depending on experience

Deadline to apply: Open until filled
       
 
   
 
 

 

As a lawyer at The Animal Law Foundation, you will play a pivotal role in advancing our mission to protect animals through legal interventions. You will work closely with the Executive Director and supervise the legal team to identify and develop legal opportunities. You will also work closely with the Advocacy and Communications Officer to raise awareness of the legal intervention. 

#### **Essential requirements:** 

**Education:** Bachelor's degree 

**Experience:** Qualified to practice law in the UK with at least four years experience, this could be as a solicitor or a barrister. Experience supervising legal work. 

This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the growth and success of a ground-breaking new charity and create meaningful change in the animal protection landscape in the UK.
