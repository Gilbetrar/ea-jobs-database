▸Communications/Social Media Internship

Are you energetic, detail-oriented and adaptable? Are you committed to the animal rights movement and veganism? Are you interested in strategic, effective communications? Are you a current student? If so, we’ve got the internship for you.
 

Animal Outlook, a national nonprofit animal advocacy organization, seeks a communications intern to assist with social media, blog posts and email newsletters as well as creating associated collateral for each. You’ll work closely with the Director of Communications to create effective, engaging content.
 

Working to end animal abuse since 1995, Animal Outlook focuses on ending and preventing cruelty to farmed animals.
 

**This position is remote and unpaid.**
 

**Responsibilities: **
 
 
1. Maintain a consistent voice across all Animal Outlook content in line with Animal Outlooks’ brand and style guide. 
2. Brainstorm and execute ideas for engaging blog posts, social media posts and other content. 
3. Create original Animal Outlook blog content covering general vegan or animal rights news, investigations, campaign launches and victories, legal work, and outreach announcements or recaps. 
4. Ensure optimal SEO and readability.  
5. Other duties as necessary. 
 

**Qualifications:**
 
 
1. Ability to multitask and work under tight deadlines in a fast-paced, remote environment. 
2. Extremely well organized, detail-oriented and analytical. 
3. Entrepreneurial spirit, strong problem-solving skills and creative thinking skills. 
4. Flexibility and openness to evolving responsibilities. 
5. Experience with social media platforms including Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube and LinkedIn. Basic video editing skills a plus. 
6. Excellent writing and editing skills across a variety of platforms; experience writing for advocacy organizations for development purposes a plus. 
7. PR and marketing experience a plus. 
8. Demonstrated commitment to Animal Outlook’s mission and to progressive causes generally. 
9. Excellent interpersonal skills and ability to interact with diverse array of individuals professionally and effectively. 
10. Performs well in a team environment, but can also consistently accomplish tasks independently. 
11. The ability to manage multiple projects at once and change direction as needed. 
 

Please note that we are only able to offer internships to currently enrolled students.
 

To apply: Please email [info@animaloutlook.org](mailto:info@animaloutlook.org) with your resume and cover letter. 

▸Legal Advocacy Internship

Animal Outlook is seeking law students for Fall, Spring, and Summer Legal Advocacy Internships. Animal Outlook (formerly known as Compassion Over Killing) is a national nonprofit organization with a 30-year history of exposing, preventing, and ending cruelty to animals in agriculture.
 

Our Summer Legal Advocacy Internships are full-time, and Semester Internships can be part- or full-time. They are remote and unpaid, though interns may receive academic credit or external funding if their law schools permit.
 

Legal Advocacy Interns will work on litigation, research, and other legal projects aimed at protecting farmed animals. Each Intern will likely work with a variety of legal claims, such as claims based on state criminal anti-cruelty laws, administrative law, false advertising and unfair competition laws, tort liability, and corporate law. Interns will have opportunities to research new projects and to assist with ongoing projects in close collaboration with Animal Outlook’s attorneys.
 

Excellent research and writing skills are required, as is an interest in exploring novel ways to use the law to protect animals. Candidates with a background and interest in animal protection are preferred. Internships are available only to current law students.
 

**Application Instructions:**
 

To apply, interested law students should create a single pdf file containing, in the following order, a cover letter, resume, writing sample, list of three references, and law school transcript.
 

Email the pdf to Animal Outlook’s Legal Advocacy Program: legal@animaloutlook.org.
 

Applications are considered on a rolling basis. Please specify in your cover letter if you are interested in a Summer Internship, Semester Internship, or both.
