## **ABOUT ALLFED**
 
### ***Mission***
 

The mission of the [Alliance to Feed the Earth](https://allfed.info/) in Disasters is to help build resilience to global catastrophic food system failure. We focus on events that could deplete food supplies or access to 5% of the global population or more.
 

Our ultimate goal is to feed everyone, no matter what. An important aspect of this goal is that we need to establish equitable solutions so that all people can access the nutrition they need, regardless of wealth or location.
 

Our solutions are backed by science and research, and we also identify the most cost-effective solutions, to save lives ([here](https://allfed.info/images/pdfs/Denkenberger-Pearce2016_Article_Cost-EffectivenessOfInterventi.pdf) and [here](https://allfed.info/images/pdfs/cost_effective.pdf)) and improve the long-term future of humanity ([here](https://allfed.info/images/pdfs/Long_term_cost_effectiveness_of_resilien.pdf) and [here](https://eujournalfuturesresearch.springeropen.com/articles/10.1186/s40309-021-00178-z)).
 
### ***Supervisor - Dr. David Denkenberger***
 

Dr. David Denkenberger received his B.S. from Penn State in Engineering Science, his M.S.E. from Princeton in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, and his Ph.D. from the University of Colorado at Boulder in the Building Systems Program. His dissertation was on his patented expanded microchannel heat exchanger. He is an associate professor at University of Canterbury (UC) in mechanical engineering.
 

He co-founded and directs the Alliance to Feed the Earth in Disasters (ALLFED) and donates half his income to it.
 

He received the National Merit Scholarship, the Barry Goldwater Scholarship, the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship, is a Penn State distinguished alumnus, and is a registered professional engineer.
 

He has authored or co-authored over 150 publications (>5,100 citations, >60,000 downloads, h-index = 36, [most prolific author](https://eartharxiv.org/repository/object/8145/download/15313/) in the existential/global catastrophic risk field), including the book Feeding Everyone no Matter What: Managing Food Security after Global Catastrophe. His food work has been featured in over 25 countries, over 300 popular articles, including Science, Vox, and Business Insider.
 

He has given interviews on 80,000 Hours podcast and Estonian Public Radio, WGBH Radio, Boston, and WCAI Radio on Cape Cod, USA. He has given over 90 technical presentations, including ones on food at Harvard University, MIT, Princeton University, University of Cambridge, University of Oxford, Imperial College, and University College London.
 
## **ABOUT THE OPPORTUNITY**
 

This is a great opportunity to do a PhD at University of Canterbury (Christchurch, New Zealand) in mechanical engineering under the supervision of **Dr. David Denkenberger** Director & Co-founder of **ALLFED**. There is no coursework, so you can focus on effective research that is related to engineering. Possible topics related to global catastrophic risks include analyzing a satellite communicating with regular cell phones, repurposing paper factories to turn leaves into protein and carbohydrate, recovering infrastructure as quickly as possible, cost and scaling of seaweed with reduced infrastructure and sunlight, reducing transmission in a super pandemic, and logistics of food and feedstock transportation.
 

If you pass the ALLFED interview stage and you are accepted at UC, pending availability of funds, ALLFED will support your PhD with **16** k NZD/year. **If you are** awarded UC’s doctoral **Connect** scholarship, you will have the tuition fees covered ([7.6 k to 9.2 k NZD/year](https://www.canterbury.ac.nz/get-started/fees/int-postgrad-fees/)), and an additional **16** k NZD/year, i.e. a total tax-free salary of **32** k NZD/year.
 

There are also opportunities to get a full scholarship plus ALLFED support for a total stipend of **48** k NZD/year. There are multiple options with varying deadlines. If your application passes ALLFED’s first stages we are happy to provide more information.
