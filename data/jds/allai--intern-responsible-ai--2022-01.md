# Apply for an internship at ALLAI

 

 
 
 

Do you see the potential for AI to have a positive social impact? Are you interested in international AI policy? Do you want to help shape AI for the better? Then apply for an internship position at ALLAI. We are looking for interns to help advance Responsible AI – AI that is lawful, ethical and socio-technically robust. We work on different projects in multiple domains, such as (inter)national AI policy and regulation, research and innovation on responsible AI and awareness building and education.
 
#### **What we offer**
 

We offer an interdisciplinary, international environment where you get to work with the top experts in the field of AI, data science, ethics and regulation. You will engage with academics, policy makers, companies, the public sector and civil society organisations.
 
#### **What we are looking for**
 

We are looking for students with a strong interest in responsible AI, who are eager to learn how to address AI’s legal, societal, and ethical implications. But most importantly, we are looking for students who are prepared to shoot for big things and are keen to have an impact.
 

We welcome you to come on board and unlock your potential. As a forward-looking organization, we are very excited to learn more about your unique perspectives, insights and know-how in this high-impact and fast-evolving field.
 
##### **Required education/skills:**
 
 
- Master student PPLE, Political Sciences, Technology Law or equivalent 
- Familiarity with EU law e.g. AI Act (AIA), GDPR and other is a pro 
- Affinity with AI and other digital technologies 
 
##### **Personal profile:**
 
 
- Flexible 
- Ambitious 
- Responsible & socially involved 
- Collaborative & communicative 
- Living/studying in the Netherlands 
 
##### **Perks for working with us:**
 
 
- Flexible duration (3 – 6 months) 
- Fun and casual working environment 
- International setting 
- Internship fee
