GiveWell is a research organization that identifies and funds cost-effective giving opportunities, focusing on global health and well-being. Our work is funded by tens of thousands of donors who rely on our research to inform their giving. We’ve grown from directing $1.5 million in 2010 to directing more than $400 million in 2025.

If you're passionate about GiveWell's work but none of our currently open roles feel like a strong fit, we encourage you to submit your general information to our Talent Pool.
 

We'll periodically review applications to the Talent Pool and reach out directly if we identify a role that may be a good fit. We may also email you with updates about new job openings.
 

*Please note that we do not commit to updating you on the status of your Talent Pool application—unlike in our role-specific hiring rounds, we do not have a set timeline or process with which we review applications.*

## **About GiveWell**
 

GiveWell is dedicated to finding and funding outstanding giving opportunities in global health and development, sharing the full details of our analysis with everyone for free. Our giving funds enable donors to contribute to the most impactful and cost-effective programs our researchers identify. 
 

Since 2007, we’ve directed over $2.6 billion to cost-effective programs and interventions. In the last two years, we’ve made more than $500 million in grants. GiveWell is one of the world’s largest private funders of global development efforts, and we estimate that the funding we’ve directed will save more than [340,000 lives](https://www.givewell.org/default/citations#Lives_saved).
 

GiveWell is most well-known for recommending a small number of [Top Charities](https://www.givewell.org/charities/top-charities), which currently support [seasonal malaria chemoprevention](https://www.givewell.org/international/technical/programs/seasonal-malaria-chemoprevention), [antimalarial nets](https://www.givewell.org/international/technical/programs/insecticide-treated-nets), [vaccine incentivization](https://www.givewell.org/international/technical/programs/new-incentives), and [vitamin A supplementation](https://www.givewell.org/international/technical/programs/vitamin-A). However, most of our research capacity is devoted to [finding cost-effective opportunities outside of those](https://blog.givewell.org/2024/03/19/what-we-fund-1-grantmaking-scope/) programs.
 

GiveWell grants have:
 
 
- Helped governments to implement high-impact health programs, like [in-line chlorination of drinking water](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/evidence-action-in-line-chlorination-india-september-2023) in India and [HIV/syphilis screening and treatment](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/evidence-action-syphilis-july-2022#:~:text=Summary,testing%20and%20treatment%20in%20pregnancy.) for pregnant people in Zambia and Cameroon. 
- Funded program delivery alongside strengthened monitoring and evaluation, as in our grants to support [treatment of clubfoot](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/miraclefeet-clubfoot-treatment-january-2023) and to [evaluate the program](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/miraclefeet-monitoring-and-evaluation-may-2023).  
- Sought to scope and scale promising interventions that don’t have clear existing implementers. We are supporting the [Clinton Health Access Initiative’s Incubator](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/Clinton-Health-Access-Initiative-Incubator-August-2022) and [Evidence Action’s Accelerator](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/Evidence-Action-Accelerator-renewal-march-2022) to identify potentially cost-effective interventions and create programs that we would be excited to support in the future. For example, we [funded a program](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/clinton-health-access-initiative-oral-rehydration-solution-zinc-bauchi-nigeria-september-2023) to provide diarrhea treatment to children in Nigeria that we co-designed with CHAI through the Incubator program. 
- Tested our assumptions through further research, including studies on [the effect of water chlorination on mortality](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/development-innovation-lab-uchicago-rct-water-quality-interventions-january-2023), [the impact of a tree-planting program on farmers’ income](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/one-acre-fund-tree-rct-january-2023), and [the effects of combining the RTS,S malaria vaccine and perennial malaria chemoprevention](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/PATH-perennial-malaria-chemoprevention-rtss-malaria-vaccine-study-february-2023). 
 

We never take for granted that GiveWell’s work is good for the world. We make our reasoning public and transparent so others can challenge it (sometimes we even [pay people to point out our errors](https://blog.givewell.org/2022/12/15/change-our-mind-contest-winners/)). We go to unusual lengths to check our assumptions and assess our impact, including [funding research](https://www.givewell.org/research/grants/University-of-California-Berkeley-In-Line-Chlorination-Research-May-2025) and [external analysis](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4294284) to address our uncertainties and insisting that our grantees conduct rigorous monitoring and evaluation. We [change](https://blog.givewell.org/2022/04/06/water-quality-overview/) our [minds](https://blog.givewell.org/2018/11/19/update-on-no-lean-seasons-top-charity-status/) when the evidence demands it.
