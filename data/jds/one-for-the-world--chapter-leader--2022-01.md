### **What is a One for the World chapter?**

Chapters are groups of student volunteers that **educate** their peers about effective giving and **motivate** them to take [The 1% Pledge](/take-the-pledge). 
  

---

 
 
### **What does a One for the World chapter do? **

Chapters organize various kinds of programming throughout the academic year on their campuses, ranging from [speaker events](https://oftw.crunch.help/semester-timeline/speaker-event-guide) and [giving workshops](https://oftw.crunch.help/semester-timeline/how-to-run-a-giving-lunch) to small group dinners and 1:1 coffee chats, in addition to regular outreach initiatives (e.g., [social media campaigns](https://oftw.crunch.help/new-chapter-guide/social-media#Link2), [informational tabling](https://oftw.crunch.help/semester-timeline/tabling), etc.)
       
 
   
 
 

 
 
### **Why should I start a chapter?**

#### Because you have the incredible opportunity to…
  

 
 

**Build a team and a strategy** that will enable you to start a conversation within your community about maximizing your social impact through giving, 

**Connect with an incredible network **of other current and former chapter leaders from [all over the world](/university-directory) who are also committed to helping people living in extreme poverty, and 

**Raise hundreds of thousands of dollars** for the [world’s most cost-effective charities](/our-nonprofit-partners) serving the people living in [extreme poverty](/extreme-poverty)! 
       
 
   
 
 

 
 
### **Who should start a chapter?**

#### A successful chapter leader is… 
  

 
 

**Passionate about helping those living in extreme poverty** and willing to put in extra effort towards this goal. 

**Excited about building a team and public speaking** because chapter success depends on your ability to recruit new chapter members and get them excited about One for the World and The 1% Pledge. 

**Experienced with time management and **[**thinking on the margin**](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/topics/thinking-at-the-margin) because we know you are busy, and so are the people you invite to take The 1% Pledge, so strategizing for your specific campus and your team’s capacity and then following through will ultimately determine a chapter’s success.

### **What is expected of a chapter leader? **

#### Chapter leaders should be prepared to… 
  

 
 

**Dedicate 2-3 hours per week** to run their chapter,** attend regular calls **with their staff chapter manager during the semester, and **attend semi-annual in-person conferences** for all chapter leaders before the start of each semester (the first in August and the second in January). 

#### **Ask friends, classmates, and strangers to take The 1% Pledge. The critical metric of a chapter’s success is motivating people to take the 1% Pledge. Ultimately, an important determinant of how many people take the pledge at your chapter is how many people you ask!** 

**Understand the **[**principles of effective giving**](https://www.givewell.org/giving101)** and the mission and programs of **[**GiveWell charities**](/our-nonprofit-partners) so that this information can be communicated effectively to chapter members and potential 1% Pledgers. 
       
 
   
 
 

 
 
### **How does One for the World support their chapter leaders? **

#### Chapter leaders can expect… 
  

 
 

**Funding** for chapter-related expenses (e.g., printing marketing materials, food for events, travel/lodging for the in-person Chapter Leader Meetings, PA, etc.) 

**A staff chapter manager** to provide leadership mentoring, management guidance, and accountability, answer any and all questions and consistently create new resources for you and your members to deploy. 

**A centralized **[**Resource Hub**](https://oftw.crunch.help/)** chock-full **of the best tips and materials from staff and former chapter leaders, including pre-made marketing and social media materials. 

**Regularly scheduled training sessions, boot camps, and roundtables** led by staff and bolstered by chapter leaders themselves!

## Frequently Asked Questions
  

 
 
-  
 
####    What if I am not sure that I will be a good fit for the role?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

Confidence can sometimes hold people back from applying for an opportunity. Here is a secret: there is no such thing as a "perfect" chapter leader. One for the World is looking for exceptional people who want to help people living in extreme poverty by promoting effective giving. So however you identify and whatever background you bring with you, we encourage you to set up an introductory call with us to learn more about how your unique background and skills can help to grow our movement.
   
  
-  
####    When is the best time to start a chapter?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

Today! If you think you might be an excellent chapter leader, schedule an introductory call today, meet Ella or Evan, ask your questions, and get the ball rolling.
   
  
-  
####    What if I do not want to start a chapter, but I know someone who might be interested?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

Send them the link to this page and encourage them to apply, or tell them to [send an email to Evan McVail](mailto:evan@1fortheworld.org) at Evan@1fortheworld.org to find out more!
   
  
-  
####    Which universities currently have active chapters?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

See the [University Directory](/university-directory) page for a list of our current chapters and Chapter Leader contact information.
   
  
-  
####    What can I do if my university already has a chapter?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

That chapter would probably appreciate your help! You can contact them via our 'Chapter Search' page, or [reach out to Ella Matza](mailto:ella@1fortheworld.org) at ella@1fortheworld.org and she will introduce you! 

Taking on an Executive Board position within the chapter is also a great route to one day leading it - and you can pick up skills and tactics that you may use as a chapter leader in the future. 

   
  
-  
####    I am not enrolled at a university at the moment but still want to help. How can I get involved?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

We have lots of ways you can get involved. Even if you are not studying right now, you can arrange giving talks at your workplace or church, or spread the word to friends and family. [Reach out to Jack Lewars](mailto:jack@1fortheworld.org), Executive Director, at Jack@1fortheworld.org to learn more about non-university One for the World programming. 

   
  
-  
####    How competitive is the Chapter Leader application process?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

If you display genuine passion for this project and demonstrate that you are a reliable and organized individual, your application is likely to be accepted! 
   
  
-  
####    Is there anything I should do to prepare for the initial interest call?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

Feel free to flip through our [intro deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hwDue8xt9MnFYRzXbOowI8lqvLUSnmz0ggOYz3umqno/edit?usp=sharing) and come to the call with questions about effective giving, OFTW, the role of chapter leader, etc.
   
  
-  
####    Can I speak to a current Chapter Leader to hear about their experience?  
 
 
 
    
 
 
 

Yes! [Send an email to Evan McVail](mailto:evan@1fortheworld.org) at Evan@1fortheworld.org and we will put you in touch with a current or former chapter leader at a similar school to your own.
