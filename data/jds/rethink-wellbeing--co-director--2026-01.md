# Co-Director

Become Rethink Wellbeing Co-Director

About the Organization

Rethink Wellbeing is a global mental health nonprofit organization founded in early 2023, with the mission to spread mental wellbeing at scale. To do so, we use engaging, proven, and low-cost programs tailored to the needs of individual communities. In 2026, our focus will be on continuing to run the CBT Lab, our online group mental health support program, while also pursuing new paths towards scaling our impact by integrating AI and serving neglected groups in LMICs, beginning with the Philippines.

About the Job

As Co-Director, you would share organizational leadership with our Founder, Inga, and help shape and implement the strategic direction of Rethink Wellbeing during a pivotal growth phase. This is an opportunity to build something meaningful and highly impactful—to take a proven model and expand its reach to serve thousands more people who need it most worldwide.

**Start Date: **An ideal start date is sometime in Q1 or Q2 2026. We have secured funding covering 0.5 to 0.75 FTE until the end of the year.

**Location: **This is a remote position however you would need to be available to visit partners in the Philippines. The salary will be based on standards for this type of position in the Philippines. Additionally there are potential (although rare) coworking opportunities in Germany, the Philippines, Vietnam, and Thailand. 

Roles and Responsibilities

Depending on your strengths and organizational needs, responsibilities would include:

- 

Strategic Growth: Developing and executing strategies to scale our programs, multiplying our impact, and expanding our global reach

- 

Fundraising & Monetization: Building relationships with funders, running donation campaigns, find opportunities to monetize our program to secure sustainable financing. 

- 

External Relations & Partnerships: Representing Rethink Wellbeing in the global mental health ecosystem and thought leadership spaces, building relationships with institutional partners, and stakeholders

- 

Program Innovation: Enable our program team to evolve our service delivery model based on evidence, user feedback, and emerging best practices

- 

Operations & Team Development: Enable our operations team to build systems, processes, and a culture that supports a growing remote-first organization

Who We're Looking For

Essential Qualifications

- 

Proven leadership experience in nonprofits, social enterprises, global health, or related sectors 

- 

Strategic thinking with the ability to balance vision with practical execution

- 

Fundraising acumen and strong partnership development skills

- 

Tolerance and excitement towards operating in the high uncertainty environments of small, young, high-impact organizations, and the resourcefulness that this requires

- 

Cultural humility and, ideally, experience working across diverse global contexts

- 

Genuine passion for democratizing access to mental health support

- 

Collaborative leadership style suited to co-directorship

- 

Comfort with remote work and leading distributed teams

- 

Availability to overlap with core team working hours – wider meetings typically take place between 11:30-14:00 UTC

Ideal (Nice to Have) Qualities

- 

Experience with digital mental health, peer support models, or scaling programs in resource-constrained settings.

- 

Background or strong interest in the Effective Altruism movement and community

- 

**Based in and authorized to work** in the Philippines with native Tagalog abilities. We are also excited about candidates in India and Poland. 

Factors like gender, race, and socioeconomic background can affect people’s willingness to apply for roles for which they meet many but not all the suggested attributes, and we would especially like to encourage people from underrepresented backgrounds to apply, even if they don’t meet all the suggested criteria.

What We Offer

- 

Meaningful impact: Shape a growing organization that's genuinely improving lives globally

- 

Co-leadership model: Share the journey with a committed founder and passionate team

- 

Flexibility: Fully remote work with autonomy over how you structure your time

- 

Innovation space: Freedom to test new approaches and learn from a supportive community

- 

Connection: Join a mission-driven community committed to mental wellbeing for all
