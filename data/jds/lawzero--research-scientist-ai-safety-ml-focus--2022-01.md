# AI Safety Research Scientist (ML focus)

MontrealApply

We are seeking an AI Safety Researcher with an ML focus, to join our team working on the [Scientist AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15657) agenda. In this role, you will contribute to our plan using your expertise in AI safety and your knowledge and experience of machine learning.
 

**Key responsibilities**
 
 
- Help clarify which AI safety research questions are central to the [Scientist AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15657) agenda. 
- Analyze proposed solutions to these research questions and develop novel solution ideas. 
- Propose, design, and implement novel ML algorithms and experiments aimed at solving or testing complex AI safety problems. 
- Scrutinize the [Scientist AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15657) agenda and its fit into a bigger strategic picture for mitigating AI risk, in order to guide and prioritize experiments to answer critical safety questions efficiently. 
 

**Required skills and qualifications**
 
 
- PhD in a relevant field is preferred but not required if the candidate demonstrates exceptional abilities or equivalent experience. 
- Research experience in technical AI safety, ideally with a focus on AGI safety. 
- Experience proposing, designing and running ML experiments. 
- Proficiency with ML frameworks such as PyTorch or Tensorflow. 
- Excitement to contribute to and shape the [Scientist AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15657) agenda.  
- Ability to work in a collaborative environment and contribute to collective research goals. 
- Ability to discuss complex findings with team members from diverse technical backgrounds. 
 

**Preferred skills and qualifications**
 
 
- Experience in some of the following AI safety research areas: agent foundations, AI control, frontier AI safety, eliciting latent knowledge, externalised reasoning, ambitious alignment schemes. 
- Machine learning research publications in top-tier venues (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, etc.). 
- Experience fine tuning and post training language models.

**About LawZero**
 

LawZero is a non-profit organization committed to advancing research and creating technical solutions that enable safe-by-design AI systems. Its scientific direction is based on new research and methods proposed by Professor Yoshua Bengio, the most cited AI researcher in the world. Based in Montreal, LawZero’s research aims to build non-agentic AI that learns primarily to understand the world rather than to act in it, giving truthful answers to questions based on transparent and externalized probabilistic reasoning. Such AI systems could be used to accelerate scientific discovery, to provide oversight for agentic AI systems, and to advance the understanding of AI risks and how to avoid them. LawZero believes that AI should be cultivated as a global public good—developed and used safely towards human flourishing. For more information, visit [www.lawzero.org](https://www.lawzero.org/)
