Role Description
We're looking for someone to join our media outreach team in London, reporting to ControlAI's Media Engagement Lead.

Your role is to expand and deepen the media’s coverage of the extinction risk posed by superintelligence and ControlAI as an organization. You'll build relationships with journalists, pitch stories, support op-ed writing and placement, and move fast when opportunities appear.

This role is highly reactive and adaptive to ongoing AI developments and media events. It requires being quick on your feet, and moving rapidly when media opportunities arise. If you want to spend all day theorising and writing long reports, this is not the role for you.

**Signs that you are a good fit:**
We care about what you can do, not what's on your diploma. Some of the strongest candidates for this role might have **worked in a newsroom, a high growth startup, a political campaign, or built their own audience** through writing online. Most importantly, you should:

- Know how media works, when a story will get picked up, and why journalists cover what they cover.
- Be persistent. You have an action bias and keep your head high even on the weeks where things aren't going your way.
- Have excellent written and oral communication skills and write well under time-pressure.
- Be honest, courageous, and stay on message even when facing push back.

**Not required:** prior technical experience or experience with the field of AI Safety. You will quickly pick up what you need to know on the job.

### Key Responsibilities Include:

  - Draft article pitches, news updates, op-eds, press releases, and preparation notes for meetings
  - Conduct daily outreach to news journalists and TV/radio/podcast contacts to book meetings for media team members
  - Spotting opportunities where ControlAI can contribute to news cycles and acting on them quickly
  - Produce daily news round ups to keep the entire team at the forefront of AI news developments

About ControlAI
ControlAI is a non-profit organisation that works with a singular mission to prevent the extinction risk posed by superintelligence and secure a great future for humanity.

Nobel Prize winners, top AI experts and even the CEOs of major AI companies have warned that superintelligence poses an extinction risk for humanity. Yet, most decision-makers and most of the public are still in the dark about these risks.

ControlAI exists to change this. We help hundreds of thousands of people understand where AI is going, and take civic action to secure their future. We meet hundreds of lawmakers to inform them about the risks, and convince them to take a public stance. We help dozens of them take action to spur a national conversation about superintelligence, and put measures in place nationally and internationally.

We do this across all democratic institutions, from lawmakers to the media, civil society, and all citizens. We operate like a startup: clear objectives, measurable goals, and real-world results over long-term research.

In the UK, we have briefed over 150 cross-party parliamentarians and No. 10 Downing Street since September 2024, with our superintelligence campaign gaining support from more than 100 British lawmakers. Our work in the UK has been covered in media outlets including [the Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/dec/08/scores-of-uk-parliamentarians-join-call-to-regulate-most-powerful-ai-systems) and [TIME Magazine](https://time.com/7213096/uk-public-ai-law-poll/). ControlAI also operates in the United States, Canada, and Germany, where we have briefed dozens of lawmakers, given [expert testimony](https://parlvu.parl.gc.ca/Harmony/en/PowerBrowser/PowerBrowserV2/20260126/-1/44292?Embedded=true&globalstreamId=20&viewMode=3) in hearings, and more.
