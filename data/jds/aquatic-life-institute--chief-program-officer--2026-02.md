### Chief Program Officer

**Job Type: **Full time

### **Job Description**

This is a remote position.

**About Us**

Aquatic Life Institute (ALI) was founded in 2019 as a hub for aquatic animal advocacy, focusing on mobilizing, connecting, and coordinating the international aquatic animal welfare movement. Placing research, coalitions, policy and advocacy initiatives under one roof means timely communication and engaged collaboration.

We are on a mission to reduce the suffering of the trillions of aquatic animals that are farmed or caught in the wild each year. Using a collaborative approach, we can generate and guide momentum for positive change. We are currently on the precipice of a global aquatic animal welfare movement, and we can ensure the work pursued in the years to come has the highest impact for the greatest number of aquatic animals. With our robust foundation in research, we have the unique opportunity to offer guidance on aquatic animal welfare, and to intervene where the humane treatment of aquatic animals is lacking.

**How the Chief Program Officer Supports Our Mission**

The Chief Program Officer (CPO) strengthens ALI’s ability to achieve meaningful improvements for aquatic animals trapped in the global food system, by ensuring that our Managing Director (MD), leadership team, and program areas are aligned, and that ALI’s strategic priorities translate into coordinated action, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes across the organization.

The CPO will own the execution of programs and initiatives end-to-end. Candidates with initiatives and campaigns expertise might be a strong fit for this role.

Serving as the overarching connector across our programs, the CPO ensures that ALI’s program delivery creates the greatest impact possible, and is adhering to our communications, development, and metrics protocols. The CPO facilitates the flow of programmatic activity, ensuring clarity and consistency in how we work, thus enabling the MD to focus on high-value initiatives such as external relations, fundraising, and thought leadership.

The CPO reports to the MD and works closely with program leads and centers of expertise. The role holds primary execution decision-making authority within approved scope and budget to achieve agreed outcomes.

This is a remote position and successful candidates' working times should be compatible with US EST or PST time zones. Some travel is required for team retreats and key conferences. 

**What does success in this role look like?**

Success in this role looks like strong cross-team coordination, clear alignment with annual OKRs, and shared clarity around priorities across the organisation. Internal communication is consistent and strategically aligned, with activities well scheduled and plans visible to the team. External relationships and partnerships are well coordinated and functioning effectively, enabling smooth collaboration beyond the organisation. The Managing Director is well supported and able to focus their time on external impact, while organisational activities, KPIs, and deliverables are clearly tracked and monitored. Decision-making is confident, well informed, and consistently helps move the organisation forward.
