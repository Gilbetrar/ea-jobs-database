###### About Sinergia Animal
 

Rated as one of the world’s most effective animal protection organizations by Animal Charity Evaluators, Sinergia Animal is an international campaigning organization working in countries of the Global South to reduce animal suffering and promote more compassionate food choices. 
 

We use negotiations, investigations, peaceful direct actions, and innovative campaigning methods to expose the suffering of farmed animals and to help build a world in which no animals are exploited for food.
 

 
 
###### About the position 
 

The goal of this position is to assist our local team in building an effective and reliable community of volunteers, activists, and supporters to help us in our mission to reduce animal suffering in Chile, Perú, and Ecuador by conducting campaigns and events. This position will involve tasks such as coordinating volunteers for various activities and events, recruiting activists for online and street actions, managing the local volunteer group, attending and organizing events for brand awareness and fundraising, and engaging with individual donors. 
 

 
 
###### Responsibilities
 
 
-  

Help build a strong and united community of volunteers and activists in different cities in Chile, Perú, and Ecuador.
  
-  

Secure a high level of activist participation in online and street actions. Serve as the main point of contact for volunteers and activists, ensuring clear, timely, and respectful communication.
  
-  

Coordinate and execute the planning and logistics of street and in-person and digital actions, in alignment with the strategy defined by the Director and Campaign Lead, including volunteer role assignment and on-site coordination.
  
-  

Lead regular meetings and trainings with volunteers and activists.
  
-  

Create, engage, and organize a community of qualified volunteers.
  
-  

Organize the participation of Sinergia Animal in vegan and related events to increase brand awareness and supporter base. 
  
-  

Engage with existing and potential individual donors via email and phone calls.
  
-  

Help with the logistics of delivering gifts to individual donors.
  
-  

Travel regionally, as necessary, to attend and coordinate campaign actions.
  
-  

Represent Sinergia Animal at public events and within the NGO and animal protection communities at large.
  
-  

Build alliances with other international and/or grassroots NGOs.
  
-  

Carry out other activities assigned by the Managing Director LatAm, and/or president, and follow all procedures set by Sinergia Animal.
