## About the Role

This position is a full-time associate attorney position with Richman Law & Policy. The Associate will work on cases brought under a variety of legal theories ranging from false and deceptive marketing of products, public nuisance claims and securities law.

Because we are a boutique firm, the Associate will be involved in all stages of a case’s lifecycle
and will be given meaningful, challenging work from day one. This is an excellent opportunity for
a new lawyer to dive right into public interest litigation.

## Responsibilities

The Associate will play an active role on cases in the firm’s practice, including:

- 

Investigations and case development.

- 

Drafting and preparing motions and briefs.

- 

Participating in hearings, conferences, and depositions.

- 

Interfacing with clients and providing guidance throughout the litigation process.

Because we are a small firm, the Associate will be involved in all stages of a case’s lifecycle and will be given meaningful, challenging work from day one. This is an excellent opportunity for a new lawyer to dive right into public interest litigation.

## About the Company

Richman Law & Policy (RLP) is a tight collective of lawyers specializing in socially conscious impact litigation.

We dedicate ourselves to serving the greater good by holding large corporations accountable for actions that threaten human health, animals, workers’ rights, the environment and our civil liberties. The driving force behind our practice is creating innovative and effective ways to influence public policy and the marketplace. Much of the work we do aims to fill gaps in the current regulatory environment. We work closely with everyday consumers and leading non-profit organizations.

RLP is an equal opportunity employer, committed to diversity among its staff and fostering a
healthy and supportive workplace.
