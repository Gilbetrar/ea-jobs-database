* This scheme provides funding for early-career researchers from any discipline who are ready to develop their research identity.
* Develop and lead innovative research projects to advance understanding in any discipline related to human life, health, and wellbeing.
* Focus on developing methodologies, frameworks, or techniques that benefit health-related research.
* Expand technical skills, build a collaborative network, and develop people management skills during the award.
