* This is a pooled fund for projects at the AIxAnimals intersection, with over $300,000 in total funding available.
* Priority categories include Empowering Animal Advocates, Strategic Field-Building and Coordination, and Actionable Research and Strategic Foresight.
* Create AI literacy workshops, develop custom GPT-powered assistants for legal briefs, organise convenings between animal advocates and AI experts, or conduct horizon-scanning studies.
* The goal is to equip the animal advocacy movement with skills, tools, and strategic foresight to navigate AI-related risks and opportunities.
