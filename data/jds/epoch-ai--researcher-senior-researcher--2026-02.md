Epoch AI is looking for a Researcher to help develop research agendas for Epoch and manage new research projects. 

About the role
We're hiring a Researcher, reporting to Senior Researcher JS Denain, to help define our future research directions and kick off new investigations into the most important trends in AI. You will also help manage select consulting engagements with external partners.

Our work takes several forms:
— **Research reports: **JS’s team oversees exploration into new topics spanning the entire AI production pipeline: from compute stocks and datacenter power requirements, to model capabilities and benchmark performance, to adoption trends and economic impacts.
— **Gradient Updates: **Our weekly newsletter offering timely commentary on important developments in AI, from the author’s perspective. 
— **Consultations**: We address clients’ AI-related questions. This includes governments, some companies like [Bridgewater AIA Labs](https://www.bridgewater.com/aia-labs), and nonprofits like [Sentinel Bio](https://sentinelbio.org/). 

Examples of projects you might work on:
— Estimating how quickly inference costs are falling at a given capability level
— Studying catch-up dynamics between frontier and open-source models
— Correlating capability benchmarks with real-world adoption metrics
— Forecasting robotics capabilities and production trends

### Key Responsibilities

  - **Pitching and developing new research projects**; kick-off new investigations into the most important questions in AI.
  - **Making estimates and forecasts** based on limited information for quantities like compute resources, power supply curves, or future capabilities.
  - **Staying at the frontier**: tracking new model releases, technical reports, and benchmark results, and identifying which developments matter most for understanding AI trends.
  - **Evaluating technical claims** to assess whether reported benchmark scores, training costs, or algorithmic improvements are credible. For example, what should we think of a lab’s [reports](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/we-didnt-learn-much-from-the-imo) of achieving IMO gold, or [claims](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/what-went-into-training-deepseek-r1) about DeepSeek-R1’s training costs?
  - **Managing multiple workstreams** with varying timelines, from rapid-turnaround client questions to longer research projects.

### What we are looking for

  - **Curiosity and learning speed**: You can rapidly absorb new information across diverse domains and stay at the forefront of a fast-moving field. You seek out new research and enjoy diving into unfamiliar topics.
  - **Estimation and forecasting skills**: You’re comfortable making quantitative estimates with limited information and reason clearly about uncertainty.
  - **Prior research experience** in at least one part of the AI pipeline (hardware, datacenters, benchmarks, AI research, etc.).
  - **Strong coding skills** for running and iterating on data analyses.
  - **Excellent written and verbal communication skills**.
  - **Nice to have**: Prior forecasting experience, familiarity with Epoch's body of work.
  - We expect great candidates might have prior experience as analysts or researchers, but this is not a requirement.

If you don't tick all these boxes but think you would be a great fit, please consider applying anyway!

About Epoch AI
Epoch AI is a research institute that investigates trends in machine learning and the economic consequences of AI. Our mission is to develop a comprehensive, publicly accessible knowledge base on AI that informs policymakers, industry leaders, and society at large.

We strive to achieve both rigor and accessibility to our work, as exemplified by some of our most successful projects, including our [database of AI models](https://epochai.org/data) and our [AI trends dashboard](https://epochai.org/trends). Our body of research includes our work on [compute trends](https://epochai.org/blog/compute-trends) (IJCN 2022), [data scarcity](https://epochai.org/blog/will-we-run-out-of-data-limits-of-llm-scaling-based-on-human-generated-data) (ICML 2024), and [algorithmic progress](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.05812) (NeurIPS 2024). You can read more about our work and mission on our [website](https://epochai.org/) and in [this Time profile](https://time.com/6985850/jaime-sevilla-epoch-ai/).
