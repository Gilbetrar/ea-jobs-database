My team is building responsible AI evaluation systems at Apple. Want to help? We're looking for a Senior Research Data Scientist (contract) who brings together human-centered research and data science expertise. Join us in Seattle, NYC, San Diego, or the Bay Area to help ensure AI systems are safe, fair, and aligned. Background needed: - Substantive expertise related to responsible AI or AI safety. - Solid software engineering fundamentals - Hands-on experience with LLMs and AI evaluation - Experience designing and analyzing studies with human subjects - Comfortable solving data engineering problems - Proven track record of rapidly shipping research insights - Drive for fast, iterative development and continuous delivery This is a contract position requiring immediate availability. Interested? DM your resume and a paragraph explaining why you're the right person for the job. While I can't respond to everyone, I review all applications carefully.
