## **Electric Sheep’s Futurekind AI Spring 26 Fellowship. [Applications now open]**

**Electric Sheep’s Futurekind AI Fellowship** is a pioneering 12-week learning, building & professional development journey. 

It immerses you in the rapidly developing intersection of AI & animal protection, arming you with **knowledge, skills, project mentorship and certifications** to accelerate your impact and boost your career.

We’ve learned a lot from our ultra-successful first year in 2025, which yielded 360 amazing impactful fellows from all over the world. In round one the fellowship produced 35 teams working on some amazing projects and building life-long connections to develop their impact. Here’s [a selection of what they produced.](/portfolio-2/futurekind-fellowship-summer-2025)

Building on that foundation, we’re excited to open applications for the ever more ambitious **Spring 2026 Futurekind Fellowship.**

### Session format

- 

Weekly facilitated discussions (1.5–2 hours)

- 

Readings & pre-session exercises (2–3 hours per week)

- 

Optional workshops, webinars, and Q&As with senior researchers and practitioners

**No technical knowledge is needed to participate and the fellowship is completely free to take part in.** If you’re unsure about your fit, we encourage you to apply anyway! We welcome participants from a variety of backgrounds.

## **As part of Futurekind you'll experience:**

🎓 The Immersion Block (weeks 1-6):

- 

Survey current & emerging AI applications with significant implications for animals, ecosystems and planet, such as decoding animal communication

- 

Gain access to powerful AI tools for animal and environmental impact advocacy

- 

Explore impactful career pathways at the AI/animals frontier

🎓The Specialization Block (weeks 7-8): 

Gives participants the chance to go deeper in one of three tracks:

- 

**Builder** — specialized classes on code and no-code tools, with practical implementations

- 

**Researcher** — writing, policy design, and strategies for influence

- 

**Career-Developer** — pathways for working with governments, NGOs, and industry actors

Fellows will choose one track as their focus but may also audit classes across the other tracks.

🏗️ The Project Block (weeks 9-12):

- 

Transition from learners to doers

- 

Prepare a final work drawing on what you have learned during the course: a concrete contribution to animal welfare & protection

- 

To support research, collaboration, & project development, you will have access to powerful AI platforms tailored for non-profits

- 

Receive tailored mentorship from leading professionals across academia, industry, & other sectors who will guide your project development & career building

#### **Horizon Sessions**

Horizon Sessions are **weekly, fellow-exclusive expert talks** exploring the intersection of **AI and animal protection**. Fellows hear directly from practitioners and researchers working in animal ethics, AI policy and governance, alternative proteins, and animal advocacy. These sessions help fellows explore new ideas, deepen learning beyond the core curriculum, and connect with people actively shaping the AI and animals space.
  

 
 
#### **Electric Sheep Coworking Lounge @EA Gathertown**

All fellows get access to the **Electric Sheep Coworking Lounge** on **EA GatherTown** - a shared virtual space to cowork, collaborate, and connect across cohorts and time zones. The lounge includes areas for focused work, project discussions, and casual hangouts, and is open to the wider EA community. Fellows can also join **weekly GatherTown socials** to meet peers, share updates, and build community.
