### Roles we have hired for previously:
 
 
- **Program Manager: **Run and help design the programs that directly further Constellation’s mission, such as the [Astra Fellowship](https://www.constellation.org/programs/astra-fellowship?hsLang=en) and the [Machine Learning Alignment Bootcamp (MLAB)](https://www.constellation.org/programs?hsLang=en). 
- **Program Operations Generalist:**  Support the many programs that we run, with the scope of responsibilities including IT management, CRM administration, marketing operations, workspace design, and facilities management. 
- **Head of Business Operations: **Cover our corporate infrastructure, including finance, legal, and HR. 
 

We expect staff to work on-site. Our office is in downtown Berkeley, thirteen seconds from the nearest BART stop. Compensation depends on experience and role, but typically ranges from $100k - $170k/year. We offer catered lunches and dinners, and cover PPO health, dental, and vision insurance. We will consider international applicants who need visas.
