## The CSaP Policy Fellowship
 

**The CSaP Policy Fellowship** is recognised nationally as a leading programme for connecting research with policy, and across government as a uniquely powerful opportunity for problem-solving, professional development, network building and access to expertise.
 

**The core Policy Fellowship** is designed to meet the needs of senior policy professionals, including Deputy Directors and Directors from Whitehall or their peers in the devolved administrations, local government, NGOs, industry and civil society organisations.
 

**In the first year**, Policy Fellows spend five days in Cambridge undertaking a tailored programme of one-to-one meetings with academic experts across a range of disciplines based on the Fellow’s responsibilities and interests.
