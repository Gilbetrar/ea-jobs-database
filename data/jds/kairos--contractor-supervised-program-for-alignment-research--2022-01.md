# SPAR Part-Time Contractor
 
 Part-time / Remote • $25–$45/hour  

 Help scale SPAR's operational capacity and deliver great programming to our community of AI safety researchers. 
 [ Apply now ](https://fillout.kairos-project.org/t/gzZLvXZWBDus)     
 
 
## About Kairos
 

 Kairos is a nonprofit organization dedicated to accelerating talent into AI safety and policy. We run SPAR – the largest AI safety research fellowship, now with 300+ mentees mentored by researchers from Google DeepMind, the UK AI Security Institute, Open Philanthropy, RAND, METR, and others. 
 

 Our fellows have published at NeurIPS and ICML, been featured in Time Magazine, and gone on to lead impactful work across top research labs, think tanks, and policy orgs. We've helped dozens land AI safety roles and supported hundreds in taking meaningful actions in the space. 
 

 We're seeking a **motivated contractor** to help scale SPAR's **operational capacity** and **support program delivery**. As a SPAR contractor, you'll provide **essential coordination and logistics support** for **high-impact projects**, providing support to mentors and mentees, coordinating technical workshops, and helping organize SPAR's Demo Day. 
  
 
## What You'll Do
 
 
- Coordinate and facilitate technical and policy workshops for SPAR mentees 
-  Support the organization of SPAR's Demo Day: source judges and attendees, develop outreach materials, help design Gather Town space, and manage the event website ( [ last year's site ](https://demoday.sparai.org/) )  
-  Meet one-on-one with SPAR participants to understand their experiences, connect them with relevant opportunities, and help assess program impact  
-  Help us organize in-person meetups for SPAR mentees (if based in the Bay Area, London, Boston, or NYC) or virtual social events  
-  Create practical guides, templates, and resources to help mentees navigate research best practices and career development  
- Taking on other small projects or initiatives based on skills and interests 
  
 
## Who You Are
 
 
 

Deeply committed to AI safety
 

 You understand the importance of AI safety work and are motivated by the opportunity to help build this critical field. You likely already have had some engagement with the field via our programs, a similar research program, AI safety university groups, or other [ AI safety initiatives ](https://www.aisafety.com/map) . 
  
 

Interest in fieldbuilding
 

 You're curious about or passionate about AI safety fieldbuilding and talent development. 
  
 

Strong organizational skills
 

 You can manage multiple projects simultaneously, meet deadlines consistently, and maintain high attention to detail. 
  
 

Proactive and self-directed
 

 You thrive with autonomy, take initiative without constant oversight, and are comfortable defining your own approach to achieving goals. 
  
 

Strong communication skills
 

 You can engage effectively with diverse stakeholders, from early-career fellows to senior researchers and policy leaders. 
   
 

 If you are passionate about our mission but are unsure about whether you meet every qualification, we encourage you to apply anyway. 
 

 You can also submit a [ general expression of interest ](https://fillout.kairos-project.org/t/gK7aM32uLQus) if you don't think you're a good fit for this role but would be interested in other future roles at Kairos.
