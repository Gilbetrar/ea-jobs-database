WANT TO HAVE A HUGE IMPACT FOR ANIMALS? ​
 

Each year billions of non-human animals are born into the human-made system of animal agriculture. The majority spend their life confined in factory farms with as little space as is allowed to maximize production efficiency and profit. In these conditions, many are afflicted with diseases or injured, either through neglect or as their bodies are pushed beyond their biological limits to physiological extremes for the sake of productivity. 
 

​
 

This is not even to mention the countless companion, liminal and wild-animals each with different relationships and interactions with human communities. 
 

​
 

The animal advocacy movement is working to provide protection and consideration for animals using a wide variety of approaches. Some groups target consumers aiming to boycott animal products reducing their production; others target the institutions responsible to reduce the cruelty of the system and reduce its efficiency.
 

​
 

There is a wide range in the potential impact of the approaches or campaign goals pursued by organisations. Carefully considered and strategically targeted campaigns have the potential to have a huge impact on the systems they attempt to reform and ultimately abolish. While, sub-optimal campaign selection can result in either failing to improve the system as much as it could have been or, in some cases, being actively harmful due to lack of research into the effects of a policy. 
 

​
 

Identifying the best approaches and goals is one of the most important decisions the movement faces, particularly given the amount of expertise and time that is devoted to achieving their campaign goals. Decision-relevant research is essential to ensure that advocacy organisations are as informed as possible and most able to strategically focus their advocacy efforts.
 
 ABOUT ANIMAL ASK
 

Animal Ask is a research consultancy that aims to address this problem, supporting the global animal advocacy movement in its efforts to oppose animal exploitation. We achieve this by providing organisations and funders with research that assists them in making informed decisions about their advocacy efforts. Reallocating existing campaign resources and talent to the areas with the greatest potential for impact, exploring and unlocking new campaign opportunities in neglected regions or markets.
 

​
 

HOW YOU WILL MAKE A DIFFERENCE
 

You will contribute to our research to help inform the strategy of advocacy groups in several key regions around the world through ongoing data collection, synthesis and interpretation. 
 

 
 

These could include industry conditions and system mapping in regions with poor government data collection. Estimating the number of animals represented in companies supply chains such as eggs sold by supermarkets or restaurants. The total number of animals alive or killed in a jurisdiction and the total number of animal life-years experienced per year. All these areas are particularly important in the global south where existing data is more difficult to work with or unavailable.
