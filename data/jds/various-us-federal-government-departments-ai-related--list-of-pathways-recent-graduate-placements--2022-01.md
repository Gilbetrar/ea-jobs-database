* The Pathways Recent Graduates Program offers federal employment opportunities for people who have recently finished studying.
* It is aimed at providing career development along with training and mentorship.
* To be eligible you must have graduated, within the past two years, from a qualifying educational institution or certificate program (veterans have up to six years to apply due to their military service obligation).
* Click "View Full List" to see all currently available placements.
