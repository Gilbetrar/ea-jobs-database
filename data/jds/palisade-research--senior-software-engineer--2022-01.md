# Senior Software Engineer

We are a nonprofit raising policymaker awareness about AI risks, aiming to slow potentially dangerous research and accelerate technical safety. We have ambitious goals and need to move fast. Come work with us! [About Palisade](https://global.palisaderesearch.org/About-Palisade-121998368b9e8023a8b7e0a59118b985)
 
---
 

*Senior role, remote full-time, your time zone is between UTC-3 and UTC+5*
 

As a Senior SWE, you will build AI risk demos and research infrastructure. You will ship features daily in a tight loop with the stakeholders and be responsible for our technical excellence.
 
# What you'll do
 
 
1. **Engineering** 
 
  - Build and ship AI risk demos in the span of days 
  - Maintain and improve our core technical stack 
  
2. **Technical Leadership** 
 
  - Lead technical architecture decisions and establish engineering best practices 
  - Mentor junior engineers and review code 
  
3. **Collaboration** 
 
  - Work closely with researchers to implement their requirements 
  - Coordinate with demo users and external collaborators 
  
 

*See the kind of work we do in [About Palisade](https://global.palisaderesearch.org/About-Palisade-121998368b9e8023a8b7e0a59118b985).*
 
## Our collaboration process
 
 
- We post daily statuses for each other to keep in sync regarding our directions. Each project has a daily sync, and there’s a weekly lab demo to show off our results. 
- We propose new ideas or directions by writing up a doc, sharing it, and getting comments. This enables async communication. 
- Our median response time to each other is in hours, not minutes; we work in an independent and self-directed fashion. Your supervisor helps you maintain direction; colleagues help with the implementation; you keep track of your tasks and milestones. 
- We offer high intellectual freedom and value curiosity. You are welcome to challenge anything we do. 
 
# What you’ll need
 
## Experience level
 
 
- 6+ years of relevant technical experience 
- Proven track record of leading complex engineering projects to completion
