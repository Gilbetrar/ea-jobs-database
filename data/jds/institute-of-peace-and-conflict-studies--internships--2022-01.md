* IPCS welcomes applications from outstanding undergraduates, postgraduate students, recent graduates, and MPhil or PhD scholars pursuing studies in Politics, History, International Relations, or related fields.
* Assist IPCS faculty in their research and programmatic work.
* Contribute original writing on contemporary geopolitical issues to be published by IPCS.
* Gain exposure through IPCS events and outreach activities.
* Be assigned to one of the four programmes based on research areas: CRP, IReS, NSP, and SEARP.
