### Internships

The SIIA internship program offers tertiary students from diverse backgrounds a unique opportunity to explore and develop a career in international relations while acquiring valuable skills. We welcome students passionate about political science, international relations, business, marketing, events management, and related fields to apply.

Internships are available year-round, with a minimum commitment of two months required for a meaningful learning experience.

If you’re interested, please prepare your cover letter, resume, and writing sample in a single document and submit your application to [recruit@siiaonline.org](mailto:recruit@siiaonline.org).

Please note that only shortlisted candidates will be notified.
